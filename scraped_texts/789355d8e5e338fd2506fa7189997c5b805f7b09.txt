Get to Know WSU Tri-Cities

Experience small class sizes and connections to professions and graduate school at an affordable, leading public research university. Washington State University Tri-Cities blends hands-on learning with opportunities for research, access to internships, personalized instruction, and an active student life.

Choosing where you’ll go to college takes time and effort, and we want to help make your path to WSU Tri-Cities easy. Learn more about the admissions process by selecting your student type below.
