APPLY FOR ADMISSIONS ONLINE

Class of 2025 and beyond, we have a message for you!

The 
Direct Admit Wisconsin

program will launch in 2024 for the class of 2025. UW-Parkside will proactively admit Wisconsin high school students, who have opted in, based on their high school GPA, academic course work, and graduate from a participating Wisconsin high school. Our goal is to open the program to all Wisconsin high schools in the upcoming years.

The Wisconsin Guarantee

Welcome to the 
Wisconsin Guarantee program
,
 where we’re excited to offer guaranteed admission to all Universities of Wisconsin (UW) institutions for those who meet certain criteria. If you’re a first year applicant from a Wisconsin high school and you find yourself in the top 10% of your class by the end of 11th grade, or if you’re a Wisconsin resident who is homeschooled and achieves an ACT score in the national 90th percentile ranking or higher, or you’re a National Merit Scholarship finalist, you can rest assured knowing you have guaranteed admission to UW-Parkside.
