Financial Aid        

Here To Help

Paying for college can be difficult, but a Lee education is more affordable than you might think, and we believe the investment will pay off. We are consistently rated among the nation's most affordable Christian colleges. Affordability has two distinct components: how much does it cost you, and how much assistance will you have to cover this cost. We know it can be complex, but this page will simplify the process, help you navigate it, and provide the information you need for your many financial aid options. 
With the exception of a few programs for which we need a separate application, the vast majority of assistance toward your college experience at Lee can be awarded based on your FAFSA information and information you provide in your Lee University Admissions Application and Scholarship Profile (both are found at the LeeU Application Portal).
