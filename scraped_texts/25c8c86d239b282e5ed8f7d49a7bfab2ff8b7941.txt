7 Steps to Start Your Journey at Bastyr 

1. Explore our Academic Programs

Bastyr offers 22 degree programs across ten areas of study. Browse our degree offerings to find which modalities and careers appeal most to you. 

Explore Programs

2. Talk to an Admissions Advisor

Our admissions advisors will be with you every step of the way, from matching your career goals to a degree program to wrapping up your application details. Talk with an advisor to develop a personalized prerequisite completion plan, learn more about our programs, get connected with application resources, and more.   

Contact an Advisor

3. Review Your Application Requirements and Deadlines

Be sure you have your supplemental documents prepared to submit with your application. Check your application deadlines so you don't miss opportunities.  

Application Requirements

Application Deadlines

4. Submit Your Application Online

Complete and submit your application using our online portal. Be sure to complete all portions of the application or processing might be delayed. 

Submit Application

5. Explore and Apply for Financial Aid

Review your financial aid options and submit your paperwork before the deadlines. 

Financial Aid

6. Follow Your Onboarding Checklist

After admission into a program, your advisor will send you a list of next steps. Typical next steps include: paying your program deposit (up to $300), completing your remaining prerequisites, and setting up your Bastyr student accounts. 

7. Decide on Housing

Explore your housing options for our campuses.
