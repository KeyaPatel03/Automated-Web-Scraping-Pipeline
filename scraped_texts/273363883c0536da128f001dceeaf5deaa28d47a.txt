Our Campuses
LOCATED IN BEAUTIFUL WISCONSIN
Here in the heart of Wisconsin, you’ll find a place like no other. From snowboarding to kayaking, hiking to bicycling, central Wisconsin is nature’s playground, inside and out. The Stevens Point campus is home to Schmeeckle Reserve, a 280-acre conservancy area right on its campus. Outdoor concerts, world-class performances, local artisans, breweries, markets, great restaurants. You’ll be amazed that places like this really do exist.

STEVENS POINT
Our extraordinary 406-acre flagship campus in Stevens Point is renowned for its natural scenic beauty, but the diverse range of cultural, athletic and social landscapes it offers are every bit as breathtaking.
