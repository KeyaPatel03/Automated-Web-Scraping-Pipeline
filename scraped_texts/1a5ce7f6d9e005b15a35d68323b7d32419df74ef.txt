Self-reported Transcript and Academic Record System (STARS)

Why a Self-reported Transcript and Academic Record Systems (STARS) Record?

Great question. Virginia Tech aims to put the applicant in the driver’s seat during the application process. STARS (formerly known as SRAR/SSAR) allows you to complete your application for admission on your terms and at your own pace.

To learn more about STARS and who should complete it, please visit this 
dedicated
 webpage.

If you have already applied and are looking to access STARS, please log into your 
Applicant Portal
 after creating your login credentials to directly access your STARS Record.
