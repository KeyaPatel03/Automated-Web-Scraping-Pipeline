Undergraduate Admission

Bryant University is excited to welcome you to our campus

Experience the beauty of Bryant's campus to discover why our students are passionate about their Bryant experience.
Information Session and Campus Tour:
 Reserve your spot for a 30-minute information session, followed by a 75-minute campus tour led by a current student. We also offer an information session and campus tour on most Saturdays at 10:15 a.m. We do not offer any information sessions or tours on Saturdays in January and June. Please use the calendar to see availability and to register. 
Campus Tour: 
Join us for a 75-minute campus tour led by a current student on Monday through Friday at 9:30 a.m. and 1 p.m. We also offer a campus tour on most Saturdays at 9:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m. We do not offer any campus tours on Saturdays in January and June. Please use the calendar to see availability and to register.
Group Visits
: This option is available for schools and organizations interested in coordinating a visit to campus for their group of high school students to learn more about Bryant and the admission process. Please submit the 
Group Visit Request Form
 at least two weeks in advance.
Self-Guided Tours
: Walk around campus on your own while following a self-guided tour route on your mobile device. Please 
submit your information here
 to receive an email with instructions for your arrival on campus and accessing the self-guided tour route.
