Making Your Education Affordable

Understanding how to navigate the financial aspects of your college education can initially seem overwhelming. However, at Bluefield University, we believe it doesn’t have to be a journey you take alone or one that weighs heavily on your shoulders. Our Financial Aid team is dedicated to shedding light on this process, ensuring it’s transparent, manageable, and—most importantly—tailored to support your academic ambitions.

We’re here to guide you through the myriad of funding options available, many of which come at no cost to you. From scholarships and grants to work-study programs and loans, we aim to transform the challenge of funding your education into an achievable step towards your future success. Let’s explore together the resources and pathways available to help finance your journey at Bluefield University.

FAFSA Filing Changes

Beginning with 2017-18, the FAFSA cycle will begin October 1, 2016, instead of January 1, 2017. FAFSA income information will come from the “prior, prior year.” FAFSA income information will come from the “prior, prior year.” Based on this, your 2019-2020 FAFSA will collect tax year 2018 income data.

These changes are being made to allow students additional time to review, submit, and update FAFSA data. They will also reduce the use of estimated income and increase the use of the IRS Data Retrieval Tool.
