Admission Deadlines:

Early Action

Application Due
 – November 1
Notification
 – By January 1

Early Decision I

Application Due
 – November 1
Notification
 – By December 10

For the above application rounds, the deadline to make changes to your application (Early Action to Early Decision or switching to test-optional, for example) is November 15.

Regular Decision

Application Due
 – January 15
Notification
 – By April 1

Early Decision II

Application Due
 – January 15
Notification
 – By March 1

For the above application rounds, the deadline to make changes to your application (Regular Decision to Early Decision II or switching to test-optional, for example) is February 1.

The above deadlines are only for submission of the Common Application. Supporting materials (transcripts, recommendations, etc.) are allowed to be submitted in the days that follow each deadline.

Spring Transfer

*If your fall transfer application was submitted by the April 1 deadline but is completed after April 15, you will receive notification two to four weeks after receipt of the completed application.

Late applicants will be considered at the discretion of the Dean of Admission on the basis of availability of classroom and residence hall space.
