International Admissions at CCU

Coastal Carolina University leverages its prime location near a top U.S. resort area, numerous healthcare providers, and international companies to offer students diverse real-life learning experiences, leading to ample research projects and internships for course credit. The university boasts a living laboratory, Waties Island, where students engage in coastal and sustainability research and marine studies in an interdisciplinary environment. Coastal's AACSB-accredited business school offers popular programs like Hospitality, Resort, and Tourism Management. Students can earn an undergraduate degree in three years and have the option to add a master’s degree through accelerated programs. Coastal truly has something for everyone, inviting students worldwide to become global citizens and improve their futures and those of others. Take a virtual tour of the campus and explore our academic colleges to see what Coastal has for you!
