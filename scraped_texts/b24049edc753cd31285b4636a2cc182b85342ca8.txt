 Tennessee HOPE Scholarship: Extended Eligibility for Graduate Studies

 The Tennessee HOPE Scholarship now offers extended eligibility, providing financial assistance for 
up to five years
 of continuous enrollment. This change opens new doors for students pursuing graduate degrees at Union University. 

 Who Qualifies?

 - Tennessee residents
- Previous HOPE Scholarship recipients
- Students who maintained the required GPA 

 Continuous Enrollment

 - Up to five years of continuous enrollment
- Funding: $5,700 annually (after first two years of UG use)
- Deadline: Recent graduates must complete an updated FAFSA and apply/enroll before the start of the upcoming semester to maintain eligibility 

 Why Choose Union?

 - Christ-centered education
- Excellence in academic preparation
- Flexible scheduling options
- Online programs available
- Tailored for working professionals 

 Next Steps

 - Explore our graduate programs
- Complete an updated FAFSA
- Apply for admission
- Enroll before the upcoming semester to maintain HOPE Scholarship eligibility
