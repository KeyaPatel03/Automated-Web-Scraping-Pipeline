Understand the Admission Process

We'll help you stay on track as you navigate our straightforward admission process.

01

Know your Deadlines

Early Decision

(applicants committing to Rhodes)
: November 1 and January 15
Early Action

(early applicants not committing to Rhodes)
: November 15
Regular Decision
: January 15

02

Submit Your Application

Submit the Common App, a transcript, a counselor and teacher recommendation; SAT/ACT scores and a video of your "elevator pitch" introducing yourself are optional.

03

Start the financial aid process

Rhodes has merit-based and need-based scholarships, grants, fellowships, and other forms of aid. 
Be sure to fill out a FAFSA application.

04

Research Scholarships

Fellowships give you a chance to participate in important study, research, creative work, and service with like-minded peers. While you are considered for general merit-based scholarships with your Common Application, these fellowships require a specialized application process.

05

Make your deposit

Our portal makes it easy to make your deposit and officially join the next world-changing Rhodes class.

06

Sign up for orientation

Prepare for your transition to Rhodes in the fall! All incoming students should 
attend orientation
, which features both virtual and in-person experiences.
