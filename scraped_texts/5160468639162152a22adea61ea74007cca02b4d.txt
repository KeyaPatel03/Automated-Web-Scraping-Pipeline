Resident Graduate

Earn your master’s, specialist, or doctoral degree at Liberty. Enjoy classes on campus at Virginia’s largest university and choose from over 50 graduate programs with more than 100 specializations, including the Juris Doctor (J.D.), the Master of Divinity (M.Div.), and Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine (D.O.). Certificates are also available at the graduate level.
