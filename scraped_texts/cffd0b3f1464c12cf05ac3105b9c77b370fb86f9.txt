You've Earned It! - Guaranteed Scholarships
We're committed to investing in your future by offering need and merit-based scholarships to students with a history of strong academic performance. We offer both guaranteed and selective scholarships. There's no application required for our guaranteed scholarships, which award the following:
First Year Students: 
3.3 GPA -$1,000; 3.75 GPA - $1,500; 4.0 GPA - $2,000
Transfer Students: 
3.4 GPA - $1,000
