Sunny Springtime | Campus From Above

We’re excited to welcome you to Marshall University in Huntington, West Virginia! Your first step toward becoming a member of the Marshall family is to complete and submit your admission application.

Information on each classification of student (undergraduate, graduate, international, and distance) and each student type is provided on this page to help you select the application that is right for you. Additional information is available on the Marshall University Admissions page.

If you have already submitted your application through our online application system and need to make a change, or if you have questions about your application, please contact Marshall Admissions, and we will be happy to assist you.

Important! Your application and all required credentials and fees must be on file with the appropriate Office of Admissions before your application can be processed and before we can render an admission decision.

Domestic (U.S.) application files must be complete at least two weeks prior to the beginning of the term for which you are applying.

International (outside the U.S.) application files must be complete at least two months prior to the beginning of the term for which you are applying.

Please note that some undergraduate and many graduate academic programs have specific deadlines. Consult with individual academic departments for application details.
