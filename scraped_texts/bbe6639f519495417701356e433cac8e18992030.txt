Graduate Students

Are you ready to take your career to the next level? An advanced degree can deepen
                        your expertise in your field or help you transition to a new career path. If you are
                        a professional or degree-holder seeking a master's or specialist's degree, either
                        on campus or online, learn more about the 40+ programs offered at Winthrop. Our Graduate

                        goals and fits into your schedule.

Adult Learners

Our Bachelor in Professional Studies program (BPS) is specifically designed for adult
                        learners (age 25+) who have completed some college credits and are seeking to complete
                        a bachelor's degree. The BPS program's concentrations - Health Informatics, Organizational
                        Leadership, Organizational Operations and Community and Health Services - offer a
                        unique curriculum with the goal of preparing students with the skills that employers
                        are seeking in today's job market.
