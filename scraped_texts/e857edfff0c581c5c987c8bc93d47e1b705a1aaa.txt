Important Changes to Federal Student Loan Eligibility as of July 1, 2026
Beginning with the 2026-2027 academic year, Federal Student Loan eligibility is dependent on the number of credits a student is enrolled in each semester. Students who are enrolled less than full-time at the beginning of the semester will see their Federal Student Loans recalculated in direct proportion to their enrollment. Dropping classes after aid has been applied to your bill in one semester will impact subsequent loan amounts in the same academic year. 
Full-time enrollment for:
Undergraduate students is 12 credits per fall/spring semesters or 24 credits per academic year.
Graduate students is eight credits per fall/spring semesters or 16 credits per academic year.
Doctoral students is six credits per fall/spring semesters or 12 credits per academic year. 

            Financial Aid Disbursements Beginning Fall 2026

Wondering when your financial aid will be paid to your bill? Beginning with the fall 2026 semester, financial aid will start disbursing 
after
 the semester begins. Financial aid will be credited directly to your UWRF billing account based on your enrollment as of the 
Census Date
, the sixth day of the semester. Changes to your enrollment can impact your financial aid, some aid may be reduced if you are enrolled less than full time. Please contact Financial Aid before dropping any classes as changes to your enrollment could result in a balance due if your financial aid is reduced. 
Please note: 
Some funds, such as Funds for Wisconsin Scholars (FFWS) and the Academic Excellence Scholarship, do not disburse until after census to allow for third party processing and to confirm enrollment. 
Accepted a Federal Student Loan offer for the first time? Before your loans can disburse, you 
must
 complete both 
Loan Entrance Counseling
 and 
Master Promissory Note (MPN)
 through 
StudentAid.Gov
.
Review your charges, pending aid and anticipated balance in 
eSIS
 under the “Billing Statement” link. You can download the statement as a PDF for full details. Regular monitoring of your billing account is strongly encouraged. The 
Billing Statement and Pending Aid tutorial
 is available to guide you.
If your aid is not enough to pay your full balance, please 
review the payment plan
 option or you can 
explore other loan options.
Processing times for private student and Parent PLUS loans are extended before the start of each semester. Please allow three weeks for the loan to be applied to your billing account. 
Email and call volume at the beginning of each semester increases, causing longer than usual wait and response times. Please stop in our office (133 Rodli Hall) during business hours for timely assistance and allow extra time in case it’s necessary.

            Avoiding Financial Aid and IRS Scams

Check out 
How to Avoid Student Loan Forgiveness Scams
 from StudentAid.Gov for important information on avoiding student aid scams. Remember, you 
NEVER 
need to pay anything to apply for financial aid. Always contact our office directly if you have any questions regarding anything you receive by mail or email.
Please note, the IRS does NOT send unsolicited emails to the public or with content that includes sensitive information. If you receive any phishing or scam emails appearing to be from "IRS Online" or containing an attachment labeled "Tax Account Transcript" or something similar, please 
do not open
 the email. Either delete it or simply forward it (without opening) to 
phishing@irs.gov
. If using an employer's computer, the IRS asks the employee to notify his or her company of the email. For more details from the IRS, please see 
Report Phishing and Online Scams.
