Schedule an appointment

Students at the branch campuses should use the contact information on this page. Solution Center staff may be able to answer general questions.

You can meet with an advisor:

By Video Chat:
 Appointments are available via custom Microsoft Teams meeting links. This helps share screens and see your advisor face-to-face. You can join this meeting through your web browser, so you do not need any additional software for this option. Review your confirmation e-mail for steps to joining a video appointment.

By Phone:
 This is a great virtual meeting option if you don’t have a webcam, or don’t need to share screens. An advisor will call you at the number you indicate, so be sure to provide the best number to reach you. Be prepared to answer a call from an unknown 414 number.

In-Person:
 You can schedule an in-person appointment at the link above. Or, you can stop by the SFSC any Wednesday that classes are in session between 9 am and 3 pm to meet with an advisor on a first-come, first-served basis.
