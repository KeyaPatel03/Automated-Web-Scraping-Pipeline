Cost to Attend SMC

COLLEGE SHOULD BE AFFORDABLE
SMC is the way college should be.

Over 60% of SMC on-campus students earn a full tuition scholarship
, meaning their state and federal college grants cover part of the cost, then we add an SMC scholarship to cover the rest. Our tuition scholarships do not cover housing, meals and, in most cases, fees.

SMC Online students pay a per-credit-hour cost that is the lowest in South Carolina among any four-year college or university.

ON-CAMPUS STUDENTS WITH A 4.0+ HIGH SCHOOL OR COLLEGE GPA:
 You may qualify for our Pioneer Promise Scholarship, which covers tuition, fees and books! You'll save $23,800 per year and only pay housing and meals costs.
LEARN MORE
ON-CAMPUS STUDENTS WITH A 3.0-3.99 HIGH SCHOOL OR COLLEGE GPA:
 You may qualify for our Full Tuition Scholarship, which covers tuition. You'll save $19,800 per year and only pay for housing, fees and books.
LEARN MORE
UNDERSTANDING ACTUAL COST VS. STICKER PRICE
Sticker price
 is the total cost of an SMC education. Very few students pay the sticker price because most qualify for state and federal grants and scholarships--which are applied the total cost and lower it significantly.

The 
actual cost
 is what most students pay after state and federal grants and scholarships are applied.

The average Actual Cost per year for students who live on campus: $
16,571
.* 

The average Actual Cost per year for students who live at home: $
3,700.
*

*Your cost may be higher/lower depending on your unique financial situation.
