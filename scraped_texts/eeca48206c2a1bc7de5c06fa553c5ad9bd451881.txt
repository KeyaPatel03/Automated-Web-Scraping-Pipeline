General Transfer Requirements

Admission preference is given to applicants who have completed college-level, academic courses covering a range of subjects including mathematics, communications, humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, and fine and performing arts. Remedial/developmental courses and vocational courses (i.e. culinary, applied technical science) are not transferable.

If you have attempted fewer than 24 transferable credits: 
You must earn a minimum 
2.8 GPA 
(on a 4.0 scale), be in good standing, and be eligible to return to institutions previously attended. You must also meet all 
first-year admission requirements
.

If you have previously been denied admission to URI: 
You must first earn 24 transferable credits with at least a 
2.5 GPA
 to be considered again for admission.

If you have attempted at least 24 transferable credits: 
You must earn a minimum 
2.5 GPA
 (on a 4.0 scale), be in good standing and eligible to return to institutions attended previously. In addition, you must meet basic college-preparatory unit requirements (typically completed in high school): 4 in English, 3 in math (Algebra I, Algebra II, and Geometry), 2 in science, 2 in history/social science, and 2 units in the same foreign language.

If you have earned an associate’s or bachelor’s degree from a regionally accredited institution: 
You must earn a minimum 
2.4 GPA
(on a 4.0 scale), be in good standing, and be eligible to return to institutions previously attended. You are not required to submit a high school transcript.

If you are seeking readmission to a degree program at URI, or have already received a URI bachelor’s degree: 
Do not fill out the URI’s Transfer Application. Visit our 
Finish What You Started
 program page for readmission information.

If you are a registered nurse with an associate degree or diploma in nursing: 
Effective February 2022, we are no longer offering the RN to BS program in-person. Please visit the 
College of Nursing 
page for more information about available programs. Note that the nursing program admission is fall semester only.

We invite RI residents who may not meet the traditional requirements for URI and may benefit from additional mentoring and support services to learn more about our 
Talent Development program
.

The CCRI Joint Admission Program

The Community College of Rhode Island’s Joint Admission Program allows you to start your bachelor’s degree at CCRI and transfer to URI. The first two years of your bachelor’s degree will be completed at CCRI while earning your associate degree. You can then transfer seamlessly into your major as a junior. Once transferred, Joint Admission Agreement students receive the following benefits at URI:

Up to 30% discounted tuition, depending on your CCRI GPA

Waived application fee

Conditional acceptance to URI

Personalized advising at CCRI and URI

More than 80 majors to choose from
