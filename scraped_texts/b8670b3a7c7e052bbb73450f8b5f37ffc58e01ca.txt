Funding Your Lawrence Education

Lawrence University is committed to making a Lawrence education affordable. All admitted students are automatically considered for generous merit scholarships and need-based financial aid. Once awarded, institutional scholarships and aid are guaranteed and renewed at the same level for four years.

To determine eligibility for need-based assistance, students must complete the Lawrence University Certification of Finances form and submit supporting financial documents within five days of the application deadline. No substitute forms will be accepted.

English as a Second Language Resources

The Center for Academic Success provides second language speakers of English with opportunities to continue to improve their English proficiency. This includes class support, ESL tutoring, ESL courses, individual consultation, and self-study using the ESL resource library.

Learn About ESL Resources

Summer Institute for International Students

The Summer Institute occurs before a student’s first term on campus and eases the transition into the academic learning community. Students engage in the types of study common in U.S. liberal arts institutions, including academic discussions, thesis-driven essays, and critical thinking about texts and lectures.

Learn About Summer Institute

Pathways Program

Pathways helps students who may need additional support during their transition to campus by focusing the first year on improving English language skills. Students in this program typically complete the required English language coursework, and their Lawrence degree in four and a half to five years.

Learn About Pathways
