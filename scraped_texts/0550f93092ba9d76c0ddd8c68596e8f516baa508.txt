View More Deadlines

Spring Transfer Deadline

October 1

Transfer Applicants
 must submit their application by October 1 for spring admission consideration. Admitted students must accept their offer by December 1.

Early Action Deadline

November 15

First-Year Applicants
 must submit their application by November 15 for Early Action admission consideration. Admitted students must accept their offer by May 1.

Regular Decision Deadline

January 15

First-Year Applicants
 must submit their application by January 15 for Regular Decision admission consideration.  Admitted students must accept their offer by May 1.

General Scholarship Application and FAFSA Priority Deadline

January 22

For maximum scholarship and financial aid consideration, 
 first-year and transfer applicants 
 must submit the General Scholarship Application and FAFSA by January 22.

Summer/Fall Transfer Deadline

March 1

Transfer Applicants
 must submit their application  by March 1 for summer or fall admission consideration. Admitted students must accept their offer by June 1 for fall entry and May 15 for summer entry.
