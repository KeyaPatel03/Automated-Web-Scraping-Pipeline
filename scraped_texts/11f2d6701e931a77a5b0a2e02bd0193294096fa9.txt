These critical steps must be completed to be considered for grants, loans, or work-study:

Visit 
https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa

to complete your Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). Beware of false sites that request payment.

Click the “Log In” button.

Click the box on the left “ENTER STUDENT’S FSA ID.” If you do not already have an FSA ID, please click the link to create one. Make a note of your FSA ID for future reference.

Log in to the FAFSA application with your FSA ID.

Link your federal tax data when prompted on the application. The FASFA requires tax data two years before the school year (2022-2023 school year requires 2020 tax data). Gather your records regarding 2020 untaxed benefits, such as child support, worker’s compensation, disability, Veterans benefits, etc.

Select New England Institute of Technology to receive your Financial Aid results, or enter our Federal School Code directly: 
007845

. We will receive your results within three business days.

Non-US Residents: have a copy of your alien registration number or permanent residence card.

The priority application deadline for Fall 2022 is 
March 1, 2022
