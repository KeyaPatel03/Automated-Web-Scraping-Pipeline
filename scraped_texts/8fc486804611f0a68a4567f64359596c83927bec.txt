Review your transfer credit evaluation

Official college transcripts are evaluated after you are admitted to the university. Credit evaluations are typically completed within four weeks of your date of admission with priority given to confirmed students. During peak times, credit evaluations may take longer to process.

You can access your completed transfer credit evaluation in 
MyUTK
 under My Resources → Banner Experience → View Academic History.

Credits That Transfer

Transfer credit of similar content may be accepted from any two- or four-year regionally accredited institutions in the US. Students from colleges or universities not located in the US should contact the Office of Undergraduate Admissions Transfer Center 
(
[email protected]
) for transfer eligibility.

Transfer credit will be granted only for college level non-remedial courses in which a grade of D- or better was earned. Many UT programs require a grade of C or higher in courses in the major.

Credits that are not acceptable for transfer include courses that are remedial, occupational, technical, or non-credit, as well as career course work used for an applied science degree from career or technical programs.

Students bear the ultimate responsibility for educational planning, selecting courses, meeting program requirements, and adhering to policies and procedures. 

The ACCP (Advanced College Credit Pathway) is a pathway program that allows advanced high school or first-year international college students to earn general education college credits to be applied towards a UT degree. This program gives international students a great start in preparing for college. UT partners with 
IPERC
, which oversees the development and administration of the program abroad. Learn more about the ACCP at 
iperc.org/accp
.

Your Transfer GPA

Grades earned at other colleges and universities are used only for admission, course placement, and other academic decisions. All courses attempted and grades earned, including repeated course work, in college level and nonremedial courses are considered in holistic review. The last attempt for a repeated course will be the one accepted for transfer credit and recorded on the permanent record. 

The official grade point average for any UT student does not include transfer grades. Prior to graduation, transfer students must have completed their last 30 credit hours at UT and their last 60 credit hours at a four-year college or university.

LD and UD Courses

Courses that transfer without an exact UT course equivalent may also transfer with the designated LD or UD.

LD = Lower Division (100–200 level) course credit

UD = Upper Division (300–400 level) course credit

Academic departments determine how undesignated credits (LD or UD) are used toward graduation requirements through the petitioning process.

Students should contact their college’s 
academic advising center
 or their major advisor for information on how to petition courses. Please note that a petition does not guarantee that the course will be approved.

Proof of Lawful Presence (EVEA)

The University of Tennessee, Knoxville, requires all students who accept an offer of admission and confirm enrollment to complete proof of lawful presence (EVEA) verification requirements.

A Tennessee law called the Eligibility Verification for Entitlements Act (EVEA) requires UT to verify that individuals age 18 or older seeking a state public benefit — such as in-state tuition, state-funded scholarships, grants, and fee waivers — are U.S. citizens or otherwise lawfully present in the United States. UT’s policy extends this legal requirement to all students. 

Most students are verified through a citizenship-confirmed FAFSA. If your status cannot be verified by a completed FAFSA, please refer to the 
Proof of Lawful Presence (EVEA) information
 regarding the documents and document submission process you will need to complete prior to the start of classes.

How do I confirm enrollment?

Log into your Go Vols Portal and click the “Confirm my enrollment” checklist item to pay the nonrefundable and nontransferrable $250 confirmation deposit.

If I decline enrollment after I pay the confirmation deposit, can I get a refund?

No, the confirmation deposit is nonrefundable.

When and how can I view my transfer credit evaluation?

Credit evaluations are typically completed within four weeks of your date of admission with priority given to confirmed students. During peak times, credit evaluations may take longer to process. You can access your completed transfer credit evaluation in 
MyUTK
 under My Resources → Banner Experience → View Academic History. 

Where can I view and accept my prior learning credits once they have been received?

Before accepting your prior learning credits, speak with your Academic Advisor to determine how your credits could be used towards your degree.
