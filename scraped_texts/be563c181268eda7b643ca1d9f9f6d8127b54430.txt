Types of Aid

01

Merit-Based Scholarships

All admitted first-year students are automatically considered for Champlain’s merit scholarships. Awards recognize academic achievement, rigor of coursework, leadership, involvement, and other accomplishments demonstrated through the admission application.

Merit scholarships range from $15,000 – $35,000 per year and are renewable for up to four years. No separate scholarship application is required.

Champlain Scholarships 

02

Need-Based Aid

A combination of grants, loans, scholarships, and work-study student employment options comprise the need-based options at Champlain College. You must submit the 
Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA)
 to be considered.
