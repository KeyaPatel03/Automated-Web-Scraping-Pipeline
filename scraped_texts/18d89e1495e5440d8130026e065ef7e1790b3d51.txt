      File Your FAFSA and Fund Your Future

Looking to receive federal financial assistance from Northern State University? Let's get started. First, you fill out a 
Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA).
 Completing the FAFSA is your ticket to federal student aid. Northern uses this application to determine your eligibility for school and state-specific aid. File your FAFSA as soon as possible to access the most amount of aid that can be awarded to you based on your financial need.

COMPLETE YOUR FAFSA

FAFSA DETAILS

October 1
The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) may be filed beginning October 1 each year.

March 1
Applications submitted before March 1 are given priority consideration for federal supplemental grants and federal work study.

      WolfPACT Scholarships

NSU's WolfPACT Scholarship is the region's most generous, four-year, merit-based scholarship. Available to first-time freshmen, scholarship money is awarded based on high school GPA and/or ACT or SAT scores.

      What Are the Types of Financial Aid?

Scholarships
Northern State University offers a variety of scholarships to recognize your academic achievements, talents, and potential. Whether you're a first-time freshman, transfer student, or returning to college, we have scholarship opportunities designed to help make your education more affordable.
WolfPACT Unleashed Scholarship

The WolfPACT Unleashed Scholarship is one of the region's most generous four-year merit-based awards. First-time freshmen are automatically considered based on high school GPA and ACT or SAT scores.
Maroon and Gold Scholarship
The Maroon and Gold Scholarship—named for Northern State University's official colors—is designed to provide financial assistance to post-traditional (non-traditional) students—including adult learners returning to college, transfer students, and other Northern students who meet eligibility requirements—as they begin their educational journey at NSU.

Grants
Northern State University offers federal, state and institutional grants. Some funds are specific to future teachers. To qualify, you must complete the FAFSA.

View Grants

Loans
Northern offers a variety of loan options for full- and part-time students, including Federal Direct loans, Federal PLUS loans and alternative loan options. 

View Loans

Student Employment
Northern offers student employment opportunities as part of federal work-study for students who are enrolled at NSU at least part-time and demonstrate financial need. NSU also offers on-campus employment opportunities for students who wish to earn income while attending college (you do not need to apply for financial aid for this).  

View Student Employment Options
