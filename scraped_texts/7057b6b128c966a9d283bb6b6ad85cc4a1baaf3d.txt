Important Things to Know

Financial Aid Disbursement Timing for Fall 2026

As we begin the Fall 2026 semester, please note an update to the timing of financial
                                                   aid disbursements this year. 

Due to new federal regulations tied to the One Big Beautiful Bill (OBBB), all types
                                                   of financial aid are now more closely tied to verified enrollment and credit hour
                                                   counts. As a result, the Office of Financial Aid will not be able to begin releasing
                                                   aid until enrollment can be fully verified after the last day to add or drop classes,
                                                   August 30. 

This means disbursements will begin Monday, August 31, once enrollment is confirmed.
                                                   This is a shift from previous years, when aid was typically released the week before
                                                   classes began, but it does not affect the aid you've been awarded. Your financial
                                                   aid remains in place; only the timing of when it disburses has changed.

View the full academic calendar, including the add/drop deadline.

If you have questions about your specific award, please reach out to our office.

Important Update: Refund Timing for Fall 2026 

We know many students rely on their financial aid refund to help cover expenses like
                                                   rent, and we want to make sure you have accurate information to plan ahead. 
Due to new federal enrollment verification requirements under the One Big Beautiful
                                                   Bill (OBBB), financial aid disbursements this fall cannot begin until after the last
                                                   day to add or drop classes, August 30. You can view this date on the official 
academic calendar
.

Official statement for reference: All refunds will be processed after August 30, potentially
                                                   leading to direct deposits appearing in bank accounts shortly after September 1. If
                                                   you have a bill or payment due before then, we encourage you to plan accordingly and
                                                   reach out to the relevant party (e.g., landlord) if you need to discuss timing.

If an outside entity requires confirmation of your financial aid status or the disbursement
                                                   process, please contact the Office of Financial Aid for any documentation we may provide.
                                                   If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach us!
