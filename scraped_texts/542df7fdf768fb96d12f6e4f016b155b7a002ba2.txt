Overview & Philosophy

Vanderbilt uses a holistic admissions review process â we do not employ cutoffs for standardized testing or grade point averages. Rather we look for students who have demonstrated strong academic skills and intellectual curiosity, and who have engaged in activities outside the classroom that have nurtured their growth as leaders. Most successful applicants have taken a very rigorous course load and have challenged themselves at the highest levels of the available curriculum. Note that Vanderbilt does not consider demonstrated interest in the admissions review process.

Access & Belonging

In the spirit of Vanderbilt admissions, we consider each application within the context of a high volume, highly selective admissions process. Every applicant is treated with dignity and respect, and we train our readers to find reasons to admit each candidate rather than reasons to deny.
Â 
We believe that a Vanderbilt education should be accessible to all students, regardless of race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, and socioeconomic background. The university continues to strengthen resources that empower all students to find belonging
, and the Office of Undergraduate Admissions supports these continuing initiatives. Our office recognizes that by recruiting students of all backgrounds from across the country and all over the world, the Vanderbilt community is strengthened as students bring their individual perspectives and imaginations to work, innovate, and create together.
Â 
We also understand that families in many areas of the world are dealing with the impacts of natural disasters, public health crises, or other events. If your family is experiencing a serious hardship in your community, please know that our thoughts are with you, and that we realize that your health and safety is your first priority. As you turn toward the college admissions process, please contact our team if you have difficulty submitting required materials by published deadlines. As is always our policy, we will be as flexible as possible regarding our deadlines, and we will work with you to help you complete your application.

Decision Plans

Vanderbilt offers three decision plans for first-year applicants: Early Decision I, Early Decision II, and Regular Decision.
Early Decision I and II
 are binding decision plans and are appropriate for students who are 
committed
 to attending Vanderbilt if they are admitted. The applicant, a parent or guardian, and the high school counselor must sign the Early Decision Agreement, indicating the studentâs commitment to attend Vanderbilt if offered admission. If the student is admitted to Vanderbilt, they must immediately withdraw applications to all other colleges and universities.
Regular Decision
 is non-binding and permits students to apply to any number of schools and to make their matriculation decision in the month of April, after all admission decisions are known.
Early Decision applicants undergo the same thorough review for need-based financial assistance and merit scholarships as Regular Decision applicants.
