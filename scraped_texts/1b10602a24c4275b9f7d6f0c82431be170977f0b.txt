The Office of Recruitment and Admission identifies and recruits talented students to comprise the entering class annually. We are looking for students of demonstrated academic acumen that possess strong records of leadership and service. We also have a partnership with Vanderbilt University.

The Office of Recruitment and Admissions does a holistic reviewing of all prospective student applications, with a high focus on:

Academic Performance (High School and/or College career)

Standardized Test Scores

Service and Leadership

*If you have previously applied via the Common Application and selected our school, you do not need to reapply.
