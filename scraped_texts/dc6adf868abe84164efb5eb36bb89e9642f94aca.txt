Need-Based Aid

The FAFSA is the federal application required to be considered for need-based aid, including grants, loans and work study.

To qualify for the 
Free Tuition Vermont
 program (for households making less than $100,000 per year), Vermont residents need to submit a FAFSA application as well as apply for state aid through VSAC.
