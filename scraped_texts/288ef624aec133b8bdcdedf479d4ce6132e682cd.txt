The Admissions Process At Coker University
The process of applying to college or transferring between schools can be confusing, or even overwhelming. We’re committed to providing all the information and resources you need to complete your application with minimal stress. If you have questions about how to get your transcripts or test scores, how to apply for financial aid, the status of your application, or anything else related to enrollment, our admissions counselors have got you covered.

06. Dual Enrollment

Coker’s 
Dual Enrollment Program
 empowers high school juniors and seniors to earn college credit by enrolling in college courses online, on Coker’s campus, or at their high school. This opportunity gives students an early experience with college coursework, thus helping them build skills and reduce future college costs.
