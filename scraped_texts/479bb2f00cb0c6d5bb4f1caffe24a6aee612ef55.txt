At Regent University in Virginia Beach, we believe a diverse and welcoming student body is the surest way to create an atmosphere where students can challenge thinking and share unique perspectives. To ensure our unique proposition of Christian leadership continues to have a powerful impact on the professional world, we aim to make our education accessible and affordable. Our admissions department has a streamlined process and knowledgeable counselors ready to assist you at each step. Regent is here to make sure you get the very best in Christian higher education with access to funding opportunities such as university 
financial aid and scholarships
.
