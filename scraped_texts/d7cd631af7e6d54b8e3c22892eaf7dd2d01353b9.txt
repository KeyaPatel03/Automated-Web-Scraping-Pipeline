Financial Aid

You may be asking yourself, “Can I afford to go to Milligan?” Let us help you change that question to, “Do I want to go to Milligan?” and then we’ll help you navigate the financial aspect of the journey. At Milligan, we’re very generous with financial aid and work hard to keep our costs below the national average. Let’s look at all the options together.
