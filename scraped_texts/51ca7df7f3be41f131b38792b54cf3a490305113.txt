There’s a place for you here

Though many of our students come to FHU immediately after their senior year of high school, others follow a different path. Wherever you are on your educational journey, we welcome you to the Lion community!

Dual Enrollment

Still in high school and looking for a head start on your college career? Students like you can earn high school and college credit by enrolling in FHU courses! Get access to our campus and online resources and help from our friendly staff. Find out if you qualify.

More About Dual Enrollment

Transfer

We understand how challenging it can be to transition from one institution to another. Our transfer support staff is here to answer all your questions about the transfer process, which of your credits are eligible to be transferred, how to get the most out of your FHU experience, and more.

More About Transferring to FHU

International Students

If you live outside the U.S., you’ll benefit from our strong, career-advancing academic programs and the support of a caring Christian community. FHU's office of international education offers special services to international students like you to provide for your needs and make you feel welcome.

More About International Students

Financial Aid and Scholarship

Financial Aid and Scholarship

We believe that all eligible students should have access to a quality, private Christian education here at FHU. Regardless of your financial means, our Student Financial Services team will help you find scholarships and other sources of aid available to you. Explore costs and options for aid.
