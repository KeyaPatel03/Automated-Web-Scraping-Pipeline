Visit the Campus

             Bennington's campus features world-renowned architecture, award-winning student housing, and 360-degree views. 

See the Town of Bennington

             Shops and restaurants line the main street of Bennington, a classic New England town nestled in the foothills of the Green Mountains.

Culture amidst Nature

             Mass MOCA, the Clark Institute, Williams College, and many other cultural offerings are in the nearby Berkshires.

Hit the Trail

             Abundant hiking opportunities and spectacular views are available in all directions.

Tour the Shops

             Nearby Manchester, Vermont, is a treasure trove of outlet stores and unique independent shops.

Downhill all the way

             World famous skiing is nearby on Bromley Mountain, Stratton, and many other slopes.

Dive Right In

             The hamlet of North Bennington is right next to campus and has a lovely pond, charming general store, and fine dining.
