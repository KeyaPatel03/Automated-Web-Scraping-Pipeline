Admissions Process

Undergraduate Requirements

              Transcripts              

Official transcripts from all institutions attended should be sent directly from the school or government agency. Official English translations must accompany any document in a language other than English.

A course-by-course transcript evaluation by a NACES member is required for all transfer students. Incoming freshman students are not required to submit a transcript evaluation.

The Evaluation Company (formerly SpanTran) is one of the evaluation services that we recommend. CSU has a custom portal with The Evaluation Company, which allows international students to obtain an expedited course-by-course evaluation with grade point average calculation. This includes a discounted rate since you are applying to CSU. You can apply with their custom application by using this link 
The Evaluation Company – Charleston Southern University
.

We also recommend 
WES
 or 
ECE
.

              SAT or ACT Scores              

SAT or ACT scores are optional for international students with a GPA above 2.5.  However, these test scores can be used to meet the English proficiency requirement.  Scores should be sent from the respective testing agency.  Charleston Southern University’s SAT code is 5079; ACT code is 3833.

              English Proficiency              

Confirmed English proficiency is required for non-native English speakers and can be met in the following ways:

TOEFL
: 71 iBT Code for Charleston Southern University is 5079

IELTS
: 6.0

Duolingo English Test
: 105

Pearson Test of English (Academic)
: 51

International Test of English Proficiency (iTEP)
: 3.7

SAT Evidence Based Reading & Writing: 530

Two years at a U.S. college or university or high school: ‘C’ or better in core subjects.

              Passport and Visa Documents              

A clear copy of the identification page of your passport is needed. The copy should show all four edges of the document.

If you are already in the United States, copies of your visa, I-94 with travel history, and I-20 may be required.

              International Financial Documents              

If you will be applying for an F-1 student visa to study in the United States, you are required to submit financial documentation to prove that you have sufficient funds for your study.

If someone other than yourself is providing your support, you should submit the International Sponsor Statement along with the bank statement. Bank statements must not be older than 180 days (6 months).

              Students Transferring From Another U.S. School              

An 
International Student Eligibility Form
 should be completed by the International Student Advisor or Designated School Official. The student must also provide copies of a current visa, I-20 and I-94 with travel history.

              Students Planning To Live On Campus              

If you plan on living on campus in a dormitory room, you will be required to submit an 
immunization form
 in order to be assigned to a room on campus.  It can take some time to get all the required immunizations, so it’s recommended to start as soon as possible.

              Scholarships for International Undergraduate Students              

All international undergraduate students are eligible to receive either the International Scholarship or a merit scholarship. These scholarships are automatically awarded at the time of admission to the university and there are no additional application items required.

General international student scholarships:

International students who have attended schools outside the US are eligible to receive an International Student Scholarship of $10,000 to $12,000 per year.  Scholarships are awarded at the time of admission and no additional application or information is needed.

International students who have attended US schools or international schools with US curriculum are eligible for academic merit scholarships of $5,000 to $19,000 per year based on GPA.  Scholarships are automatically awarded when admitted to the university.

Special scholarship opportunities:

Global Ambassador Scholarship
—One full tuition scholarship is awarded each year to an international student whose parents serve in a Christian ministry position outside the United States.

To apply for this scholarship you must be an admitted student and submit all application requirements, which include a resume listing your activities and accomplishments, two letters of recommendation, and a personal essay describing your family background in ministry. The university awards this scholarship at its discretion on a rolling basis. Priority is given to applications that are completed by December 1st. To begin the application process, please complete the 
online form
 to confirm your interest.

Admissions Deadline

The deadline for admission for international students is 60 days prior to the first day of classes of the academic term of entry. If you have questions about the admission deadline, please contact the 
Enrollment Services Office
 at 843-863-7050 or 
international@csuniv.edu
.

Once You Are Accepted

When an applicant has been accepted by Charleston Southern University, and financial documentation has been submitted, a Form I-20 Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status will be mailed along with an acceptance letter. This form will need to be taken to the American Consulate or Embassy in the student’s home country for the F-1 visa to be issued.
