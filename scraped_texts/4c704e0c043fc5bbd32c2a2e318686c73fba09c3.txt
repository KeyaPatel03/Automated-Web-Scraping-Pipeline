YOU CAN AFFORD CARSON-NEWMAN

We are committed to keeping our top-quality Christian liberal arts education affordable for our students.
 Our tuition and fees are in line with or lower than similar colleges, and we offer a comprehensive range of scholarships, grants, loans, and other assistance based on financial need, exceptional academic ability, and special talents.
