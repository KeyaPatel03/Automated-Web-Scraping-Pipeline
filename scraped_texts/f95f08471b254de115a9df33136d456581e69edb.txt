What Kind of Student Are You?

First-Time Freshman

Are you a high school senior or recent graduate thinking about making APSU your home? Discover more about our simple admissions process.

Transfer

You are considered a transfer student if you have attempted 12 or more college credit hours. Let us help you make this transition as easy as possible.

Military-Affiliated

As a service member, veteran or military veteran family member, we want to thank you and support you. Explore the resources available for you.

International

If you are seeking an undergraduate degree as a non-U.S. citizen or as a F-1 or J-1 visa holder, view your specific application requirements and international admissions support team.

Dual Enrollment

Are you a high school student looking to earn college credit before you graduate? Learn more about our GovNow dual enrollment program.
