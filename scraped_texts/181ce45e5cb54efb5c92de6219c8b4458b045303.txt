Annual Security and Fire Safety Report
| Old Dominion University is very committed to assisting all members of the community in providing for their own safety and security. As required by federal law The Jeanne Clery Disclosure of Campus Security Policy and Campus Crime Statistics Act, 20 U.S.C 1092(f), known as the "Clery Act"), the university publishes an 
Annual Security and Fire Safety Report
, which contains information regarding campus security, crime reporting polices, personal safety and crime prevention, and fire and crime statistics for our various campus locations. To request a paper copy of the current report, please contact the ODU Police Department by calling (757) 683-4000 or in person at 4516 Monarch Way, Norfolk, Virginia 23508.
