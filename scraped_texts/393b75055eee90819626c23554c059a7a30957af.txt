Contact Tracing

Anderson University is developing contact tracing protocols in the event a member of the campus community contracts COVID-19. 

The AU COVID-19 Medical Group and Thrive Wellness Center is partnering with the South Carolina Department of Health and Environmental Control (DHEC) epidemiological services teams to develop and institute a COVID-19 contact tracing process and reporting system.

All students who are probable COVID-19 cases (with symptoms of COVID-19) or confirmed COVID-19 cases (tested positive for COVID-19) should contact the Thrive Wellness Center to facilitate contact tracing. 

All AU employees (faculty and staff) who are probable or confirmed COVID-19 cases should contact their respective supervisor and the AU Human Resources (HR) Department to initiate contact tracing.
