First-Year Applicants

If any of the following apply to you, follow the instructions to apply as a 
first-year student
:

Currently in high school, even if taking college courses.

Participating in a dual-enrollment program, even if you’re earning enough credits for an associate’s degree. (Dual-enrollment means that you are in high school and also in a college-level program.)

Graduated with a high school diploma, GED, or high school diploma equivalency test and have not enrolled in a degree-seeking program at a college or university.

Transfer Applicants

If you have graduated with a high school diploma, GED, or high school diploma equivalency test and if any of the following apply to you, follow the instructions to apply as a 
transfer student:

You are currently or have been enrolled as a degree-seeking student at a college or university that is regionally accredited in the U.S. 

You are currently or have been enrolled as a degree-seeking student at a college or university outside the U.S. that is nationally recognized by the country's primary accrediting council/agency. 

You are currently or have been enrolled in a non-degree-seeking program in the U.S. and will have completed 24 semester credits or more by the time you plan to enroll at Bennington College.

If you’re unsure of your degree-seeking status or accreditation of your most recent school, we recommend you contact your school’s registrar’s office.

Reapplying to Bennington

Bennington College does not accept first-year applications from students who have previously applied and were denied admission. Before re-applying to Bennington, the applicant must complete one year of undergraduate study at another college or university and then apply as a Transfer applicant.

If you previously started an application but did not complete it, withdrew your application, or were admitted but did not enroll, contact the Admissions Office at 
admissions@bennington.edu
 to confirm the appropriate next step.

Bennington Young Creators Awards

             The Bennington Young Creators Awards (YCA) recognize high school students in grades 9-12 who demonstrate originality, curiosity, and independent thinking through writing, visual art, music, innovation, and science. Each Award is free to enter and offers cash prizes up to $2,000, recognition, publication, or showcase opportunities, and scholarships for students who enroll at Bennington College.

Students currently enrolled in Grade 12 should submit their entry through the Common Application or the Coalition Application. After you apply, you will receive instructions via email to complete your Young Creators Awards entry. There is no application fee, supplemental essay, or binding commitment, allowing you to apply to Bennington College and enter the contest through a single process.

Application Authenticity:
 At Bennington College, authenticity, integrity, and intellectual honesty are central to our admissions process, which reflects the values of our community. We expect our applicants to submit original applications and materials that are truthful, accurate, and directly representative of the applicant’s own experiences.
