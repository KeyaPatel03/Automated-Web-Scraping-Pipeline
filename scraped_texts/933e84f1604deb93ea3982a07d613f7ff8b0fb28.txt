Welcome, Future Bears! Your Journey Begins Here

At Southern Adventist University, we’re excited to help you combine your passions
                                       with God's purpose. As you begin this new chapter, you’ll join a faith-based community
                                       of individuals who are driven to succeed and also committed to making a difference
                                       in the world. You'll discover how God’s plans can transform and guide your path academically,
                                       spiritually, and beyond life at Southern. We are dedicated to supporting you in your
                                       personal growth, academic excellence, and faith journey. Your path to success begins
                                       now and it starts here.
