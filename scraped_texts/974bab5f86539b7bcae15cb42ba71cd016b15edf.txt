Quick Application Types

These quick links will take you directly to each admissions category.

Freshman

Freshman

Please complete the freshman application if you are currently enrolled in your last (senior) year of high school, or you have graduated high school but not attended a college or university yet. If you have taken college classes while still enrolled in high school, you are still considered a first-year freshman.

Learn more about Freshman Admissions

Transfer or Second Degree

Transfer or Second Degree

If you have previously attended another institution and are interested in completing your degree at Coastal Carolina, then you will apply as a Transfer Student or Second Degree.

Learn about Transferring to Coastal

Non-Degree

Non-Degree

A non-degree student is someone who is taking classes at the University but not pursuing a degree. The non-degree admissions classification allows certain students the opportunity to begin classes prior to providing the finalized credentials necessary to enroll as a degree-seeking student. Applicants denied admission into a degree-seeking program at Coastal Carolina are not eligible to apply as a non-degree student. 

Discover the different Non-Degree application types

Re-Admit

Re-Admit

A re-admit student is someone who was enrolled at Coastal Carolina University as a degree-seeking student and missed a major term (fall or spring semester).
