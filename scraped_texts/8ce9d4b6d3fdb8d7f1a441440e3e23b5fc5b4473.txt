Frequently Asked Questions

            Is there a waitlist at Bryant & Stratton College?

Bryant & Stratton College does not operate on a traditional waitlist system. Admissions are reviewed on a rolling basis, so we encourage you to apply as early as possible to ensure you have enough time to complete both the admissions and financial aid processes before your intended start date. Because our programs start every 8–16 weeks, there are multiple opportunities throughout the year to begin your studies.

 or

            I am not sure what I want to study. Can Bryant & Stratton College help?

Absolutely! You don’t need to have everything figured out before you reach out to us. Our admissions advisors are trained to help you explore your interests, strengths, and career goals to identify programs that could be a great fit. Bryant & Stratton College offers 45+ degree programs across eight areas of study, including business, healthcare, nursing, technology, education, and more, so there are plenty of paths to discover.             Are Bryant & Stratton College credits transferable?

Bryant & Stratton College accepts transfer credits from other accredited institutions, and B&SC credits may transfer to other colleges depending on the receiving institution’s policies. When you provide your transcripts, our Transfer Coordinator will conduct a preliminary evaluation to determine how your previous coursework applies toward your program requirements. Students may earn a maximum of 75% (70% in Virginia) of the credits required for a degree through transfer. Because transfer policies vary by institution, we recommend checking with your target school directly as well.
