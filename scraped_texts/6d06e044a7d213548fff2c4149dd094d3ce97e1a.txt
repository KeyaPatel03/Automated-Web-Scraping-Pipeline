Confirm Your Enrollment

Log into your 
Go Vols Portal
 and navigate to the “Next Steps” tab. Click on the “Confirm your enrollment,” checklist item and pay the $250 deposit online. The deposit is nonrefundable and nontransferable, but the great news is that it will be credited toward your first semester costs.

Admitted Students FAQs

Below are answers to some frequently asked questions about submitting test scores and transcripts once you are an admitted student. Remember, our team is always here to help if you have further questions. Give us a call at 
865-974-1111
 or 
submit a case
.

How do I confirm enrollment?

Log into your Go Vols Portal and click the “Confirm my enrollment” checklist item to pay the non-refundable and non-transferrable $250 confirmation deposit.

If I decline enrollment after I pay the confirmation deposit, can I get a refund?

No, our confirmation deposit is non-refundable.

Why do I need to submit my final high school transcript and/or official test scores?

It is important for the university to receive your final high school transcript and/or official test scores so we can verify the information submitted on your application for admission. Incorrect information can affect things like your financial aid or even your admission as a whole.

I submitted my high school transcript and test scores on my Go Vols Portal. Why do I have to do it again?

Undergraduate Admissions must be able to verify information you self-reported on your application using your final high school transcript and official test scores to finalize your financial aid and admission to UT.

What happens if I do not submit my final high school transcript and/or official test scores?

If we do not receive your final high school transcript and official test scores, you will not receive scholarships or financial aid and risk losing your admission status.

How do I turn in my final high school transcript?

Your final high school transcript will need to come directly from your high school guidance counselor.

Send with your final high school transcript. You can ask your school counselor to attach your official test scores to your transcript so they’re bundled together.

What if I can’t turn in my high school transcript by July 1?

If you know that your transcript won’t be available prior to July 1, please follow the below steps:

Log into your 
Go Vols Portal
.

Navigate to the “GPA & Test” tab.

Fill out the form under “Delayed Final High School Transcript Submission.”

How do I know when my transcript and official test scores have arrived?

You will know we have received your final high school transcript when the checklist item on your 
Go Vols Portal
 under “Next Steps” has a green checkmark next to it.
You will know we have received your official test scores when you navigate to the “GPA & Test” tab on your 
Go Vols Portal
 and see a “Verified” next to your submitted test scores.
