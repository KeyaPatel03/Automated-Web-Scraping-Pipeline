When Should I apply to Graduate School?

Apply as early as possible. Some programs have priority deadlines, limited cohort sizes or additional review processes that can affect admission availability. Applying early also gives you the best opportunity for admission, assistantships and financial aid consideration when available.

Accelerated Master's Programs (AMP) 
Undergraduate Students
Accelerated Master's Programs (AMP) at UW-Green Bay are designed for students interested in pursuing a graduate degree program upon completion of their baccalaureate degree. Undergraduate students accepted into select programs may take graduate-level courses that fulfill requirements for both an undergraduate and graduate degree at UW-Green Bay.

Can I be re-admitted to a graduate program?
Maybe. Re-admission is available for some UW-Green Bay graduate programs, but not all programs permit re-admission. Program-specific policies may apply. If you're unsure whether your program is eligible, contact the program directly or the Office of Graduate Studies.
If you previously completed coursework in a UW-Green Bay graduate program, want to return to the same program, and have not taken a class for 1 to 3 consecutive terms, you may be eligible for re-admission without completing the full application process. To be considered, complete the

Discontinued Student Re-Admit Form
.
