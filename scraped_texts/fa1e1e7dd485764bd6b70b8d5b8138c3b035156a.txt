Ready to Rise? Make your way here.

You can see Christ
 in the Blue Ridge Mountains. You can feel Him when a professor asks you “how’s life?” You’ll know Him as your faith grows deeper and connects to your life, your career and your work. North Greenville University transforms the student experience into a leadership experience–and we have the perfect vantage point to do it.

With the Blue Ridge Mountains

outside your window, outdoor adventure is endless. Additionally, Greenville is one of the fastest-growing tech hubs in the country. Bloomberg called us “the Silicon Valley of the South.” Whatever you’re passionate about, you can learn about it and do it here.
