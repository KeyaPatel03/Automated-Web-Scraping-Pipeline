Why The University of Wyoming?

                                 											Strong Academics

The University of Wyoming offers more than 200 areas of study, giving students the
                                          opportunity to explore diverse fields and discover their true passions. At UW, we
                                          believe in empowering students to pursue their interests with purpose and dedication.
                                          Our university's size allows for unique advantages, including more opportunities for
                                          hands-on learning, in-depth research experiences and closer connections with faculty
                                          members. This student-centered approach to education is a cornerstone of UW. It empowers
                                          our students to prepare for success and make an impact that extends far beyond our
                                          campus.

                                 											Affordable

“We value access to an affordable, high-quality education.” This is the very first
                                          line of our 
Values and Propositions
 because we wholeheartedly stand by this sentiment. We know that Wyoming is full of
                                          hardworking and driven individuals who can have an immense impact on making their
                                          community, their state, and the world a far better place. At UW, we are committed
                                          to breaking down financial barriers by offering competitive tuition rates, generous
                                          scholarships and financial aid options.

                        							First-Year Students

Starting at the University of Wyoming is an exciting experience! With exceptional
                                       academic programs and a vibrant, welcoming community, students are drawn to UW. Our
                                       admissions team is here to guide you every step of the way, ensuring you have everything
                                       you need for a smooth and successful transition to campus life.

First-Year Student Admissions

Transferring to the University of Wyoming is an exciting step forward in your academic
                                       journey! At UW, transfer students are not only welcomed but also set up for success.
                                       The UW admissions team can help you transition smoothly, providing guidance through
                                       the entire admissions process. Whether you’re bringing in credits from another school
                                       or changing direction, our team is here to help.

Transfer Student Admissions

UW offers a diverse range of prestigious graduate programs, and we're excited to guide
                                       students through the admissions process. While there are general requirements for
                                       all grad students, each program has its own specific criteria. Fortunately, our UW
                                       admissions team has the resources and has expert staff to support you every step of
                                       the way.

Graduate Student Admissions

Navigating the admissions process as an international student can feel daunting, but
                                       we’re here to make it easier! From ensuring you have the right documentation to connecting
                                       you with expert advisors, our team is dedicated to supporting you every step of the
                                       way. At UW, you’ll always have the help you need to make your transition smooth and
                                       successful.

International Student Admissions

From military veterans to non-degree-seeking students, there’s a path for everyone
                                       at UW. Our team is here to support your unique journey and connect you with the resources
                                       you need to succeed. No matter your background or goals, you’ll find the guidance
                                       and community to help you thrive.
