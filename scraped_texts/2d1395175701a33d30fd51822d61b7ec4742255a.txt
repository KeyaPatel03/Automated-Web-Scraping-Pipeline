Travel Reimbursement

Visiting from a distance? Check out our Travel Reimbursement option!

Reimbursement is available (up to $500) to those future DWU students who fly to South Dakota to visit Dakota Wesleyan University prior to
                enrolling.

This offer does not apply for the flight to campus to start school.

Student needs to be at least a high school junior at time of visit.

Student can choose to fly into Sioux Falls, Minneapolis or Omaha. Arrangements can be made for pick-up in Sioux Falls by DWU staff with sufficient
                notice.

Reimbursement is only available to the student – not available for the parent accompanying the student.

Reimbursement is only available for air travel.

At the time of enrollment, credit (up to $500) will be given with proper receipt and paperwork presented to the DWU Admissions Office. Student must be
                enrolled as full-time. There is no cash value for the credit.

All campus visit arrangements must be made through the Admissions Office and at least a one-week prior notice of visit must be made to redeem offer.

        Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How long should I plan for my visit?

        Campus visits take a minimum of two hours. If you want to meet with athletics or performing arts, talk with Financial Aid, meet with Campus Pastors, or eat lunch, please plan for longer.

Where do I park?

        Parking on campus is FREE, and you will have a reserved parking spot. We will send full driving and parking directions with your visit itinerary that includes a specific address to type into
        your Maps app.

Who all can I meet with? What all can I do on my visit?

        You tell us! A standard campus visit involves meeting with your Admissions Counselor, meeting with a professor in your subject area, and a campus tour. In addition, you can request to meet
        with or audition for Performing Arts, visit with athletics, and more! Check all the options you like when you complete the visit form.

I requested a visit. When will I get my full itinerary?

        Visits are planned specially for YOU. You will receive your itinerary at least 2 days prior to your scheduled visit date.

I got my itinerary, but something I wanted to do isn't listed.

        We try our best to make your time on campus the most fulfilling it can be! Simply reply to your visit itinerary email if you have questions or if you want to add to your visit.

What if I have to reschedule?

        No problem, life happens! Just text your Admissions Counselor or email the 
Campus Visit Coordinator
.

I want to take in a sporting or performing arts event when I'm there. Can I?

        Yes! If you'd like to attend any athletic event or concert, play, or performance while you're here, simply let us know and we will provide you with free passes.

I'm coming from out of town. Any lodging and dining recommendations?

        Mitchell has many great restaurants and hotels. Check out the 
Mitchell Visitors website
. Mention you're visiting DWU when booking
        your hotel for possible discounts!

What if my interests change before my visit?

        Did you sign up as a Business major but recently decided to study Education, for example? If you are considering a different major or activity, let us know and we'll update your professor
        visit and add or subtract any other necessary items to your itinerary! Reach out to our 
Campus Visit Coordinator
.

Can I do a virtual tour?

        Yes! We offer virtual visits where you can meet with your admissions counselor on Zoom. If you want to just look around, you can also explore our 
virtual tour videos
.
