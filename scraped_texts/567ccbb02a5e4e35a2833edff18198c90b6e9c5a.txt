Choose West Liberty
West Liberty University is a place where you can pursue your dreams and prepare for the future. With 70 majors, a vibrant campus life, small classes, and an affordable cost, the quality education you’ll receive at West Liberty is like no other. West Liberty graduates renowned surgeons, accomplished artists, and award-winning entrepreneurs. The professors teach from the heart and are passionate about helping each student excel.
