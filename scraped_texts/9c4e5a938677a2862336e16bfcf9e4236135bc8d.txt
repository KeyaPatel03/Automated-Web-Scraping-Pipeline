Transfer students are an important part of the UW community.

We offer several ways to learn about the transfer process, application requirements, academic programs, and student experience. To connect with admissions counselors, consider attending one or more of the following events.

Events for prospective transfer students

Visit UW in person at a 
Transfer Thursday
. Transfer Thursdays include an on-campus tour and information about the transfer admissions process, campus resources, and student life.

Connect online through virtual 
Transfer Webinars
. These sessions cover topics such as applying to UW, preparing a competitive application, and life as a transfer student. Many webinars feature specific academic departments, so be sure to review the schedule and consider sessions related to your intended major.

Experience UW firsthand at the 
Transfer Student Preview event
. This annual event is a special on-campus event designed for prospective transfer students. Learn about admissions, academics, campus resources, and what to expect as a UW transfer student.

College Academic Distribution Requirements

Admission to the UW requires completion of coursework in the subjects listed below — whether in high school, college or a combination of the two — unless you have earned 40 transferrable college quarter credits or more at the time of application.
