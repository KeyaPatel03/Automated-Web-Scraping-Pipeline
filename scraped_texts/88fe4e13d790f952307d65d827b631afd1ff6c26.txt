At the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga, graduate education goes beyond the classroom. Backed by dedicated faculty, cutting-edge research, and a city known for its innovative spirit and charm, UTC's graduate programs are designed to challenge and inspire — giving you the enriching learning experiences you need to ignite your passion, advance your career, and make a lasting impact.
Whether you're a recent graduate or a working professional, UTC's Graduate School offers a wide range of master's, doctoral, and specialist programs spanning a broad spectrum of fields and careers. 
UTC is ready to meet you where you are — with flexible program options, an affordable cost of attendance, and outstanding faculty committed to one-on-one mentorship and personalized support. Your next step starts here!
