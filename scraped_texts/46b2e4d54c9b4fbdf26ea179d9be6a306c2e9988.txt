How to apply to St. Norbert College

St. Norbert College offers rolling admission, so you can 
apply
 at any time and there is no strict application deadline. Once your application is complete, you can expect to receive an admission decision within two to four weeks. St. Norbert College welcomes applications from first-year, transfer, international and graduate students. Use the links below to find application requirements, deadlines and next steps for your student type.

At St. Norbert College, we understand that you are more than a test score. If your application doesn’t fully reflect your experiences, we encourage you to submit a résumé to highlight your accomplishments, leadership and involvement. If you have questions about the application process, our 
admissions counselors
 are here to help guide you every step of the way.
