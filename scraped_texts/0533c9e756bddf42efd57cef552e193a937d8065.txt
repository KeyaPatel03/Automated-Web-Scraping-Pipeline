Student Financial Services

Each year, CSU awards more than $80 million in financial aid. Merit-based awards ranging up to $17,000 and other scholarships can make attending CSU a reality. A combination of merit-based and need-based aid means that CSU is more affordable than you may think.

Our team of dedicated professionals will help put together a financial aid package that is tailored to you and support you through the process. We want you here, and are committed to serving you by providing financial resources that help you meet your educational goals.

How to Apply for Financial Aid

Submit the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) to begin the process of applying for financial aid. Make sure to enter CSU’s school code: 003419.

We’ve simplified the rest of the process with helpful steps and deadlines to make it easy to navigate.

Spotlight on Scholarships

              Buc Promise  – Tuition Promise Program              

Students from all economic backgrounds who have shown grit and intellect deserve the opportunity to attend CSU. 
This is our promise: if a student is admitted to CSU and qualifies for Buc Promise, CSU will pay their tuition.

Buc Promise guarantees full tuition will be covered by a combination of federal, state, and institutional aid for eligible South Carolina residents. You will not have to pay back this aid. You can also be assured that if tuition increases and you remain eligible, the financial aid you receive will increase as well.

              Palmetto Academic Excellence Award

Full tuition is covered for all four years, plus housing and meal costs for sophomore, junior, and senior year.

Students from all economic backgrounds who have shown grit and intellect deserve the opportunity to attend CSU. You will not have to pay back this aid. You can also be assured that if tuition increases and you remain eligible, the financial aid you receive will increase as well.

Types of Financial Aid

Scholarships

Many scholarship awards are what we refer to as merit-based. They are based on your GPA and do not need to be repaid. 97% of full-time freshmen receive a merit-based scholarship. Additional CSU scholarships, for athletics, fine arts and more are available for students based on eligibility.

Grants

Several grant programs are available to qualifying CSU students. Unlike loans, grants are based on need and qualifications, and do not have to be paid back.

Loans

Student loans are based on need and determined by the information you submit on the FAFSA. These funds, along with interest, do need to be repaid.

On-Campus Employment

Students may qualify for on-campus employment during their time at CSU through our federal work-study program. This is determined by the information you submit on the FAFSA. Earnings are paid directly to students and do not need to be repaid.
