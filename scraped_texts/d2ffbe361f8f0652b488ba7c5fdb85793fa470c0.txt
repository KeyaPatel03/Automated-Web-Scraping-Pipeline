Your First Year Financial Aid Offer

PUT YOUR FUTURE WITHIN REACH

We are here to empower you. At Ripon, your financial circumstances will never affect our admission decision. Our Office of Financial Aid is here to get to know your story. Assess your needs. Answer your questions. Together, we can explore merit-based scholarships, need-based grants, educational loans, work-study and more as options to customize an affordable path forward.

Ready to get started? Contact our Office of Financial Aid at 920-748-8101 or
