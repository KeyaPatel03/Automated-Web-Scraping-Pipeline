At UW-Stout Polytechnic, you’ll do more than earn an education—you’ll do on day one
. In as early as your first week of class, you'll develop the skills and experiences necessary to thrive in today's dynamic professional world. From innovative labs and studios to industry partnerships, you'll have unmatched opportunities to engage in real-world scenarios and gain invaluable applied learning and research experience. And when you graduate, you'll have with the confident, credentials, and expertise to excel in your chosen field.
