Do I Qualify for Financial Aid?  

At Central, we encourage all students to apply for financial aid—whatever their income level. 

Don’t assume you’re not eligible because of your or your family’s financial situation. Various need-based and non-need-based programs are available to supplement your resources.

To be eligible for federal, state, or need-based institutional aid at CWU, you must complete
 the 
Free Application for Federal Student Aid
 (FAFSA)

or 

Washington Application for State Financial Aid
 (WASFA). Once your information is received by CWU, we’ll use the information provided to determine your eligibility for financial aid. 

How to Pay for College with Different Aid Options

Before you embark on the financial aid process, it’s essential to understand the different forms of aid, their repayment stipulations, and more. Be sure to review CWU’s available types of financial assistance.

Scholarships:

Scholarships are an excellent source of funding that helps lower the cost of your education and does not require repayment. CWU’s Scholarship Central houses a broad range of merit, athletic, academic, and other scholarships.

Grants:

Unlike loans, grants typically do not have to be repaid. Discover more about federal and state grants, institutional gift aid, and the Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE) scholarship to determine if you qualify.

Work-Study Programs:

Work-study programs are a form of financial assistance that helps you get a part-time job to pay for college while gaining essential work experience. Your financial assistance letter will reflect how much you can earn through your work-study program.

Loans:

Federal and private loans are popular options for students investing in their education. Check out all the different loan options, eligibility requirements, interest rates, and more.

Accepting Your Financial Aid Offer

Once accepted to CWU, you will receive your financial aid offer in the mail, notifying you that you have been awarded financial aid. Your 
financial aid award letter
 outlines what forms of aid you will receive, your cost of attendance, and more. 

After reviewing your aid offer, you can accept, decline, or reduce your specific awards on 
MyCWU
.

Understanding Cost of Attendance 

Cost of attendance (COA)
 is your total expense to attend Central. This approximation includes both direct and indirect costs and will vary based on whether you’re a resident or non-resident, if you live on campus, and additional factors.

Our 
Net Price Calculator
 helps you estimate the cost of your CWU education and the financial aid package you may receive. Please note: this is an estimate, so the COA in your financial aid offer may differ.
