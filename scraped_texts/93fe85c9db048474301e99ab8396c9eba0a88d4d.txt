ACT/SAT Test Optional

At Dakota Wesleyan University, you do not have to submit your ACT or SAT test scores with your application.

DWU aims to remove barriers that may impede
        some students’ ability to pursue a Dakota Wesleyan education. With that aim, DWU does not require first-year students to submit their standardized test scores as a part of their
        application. There are exceptions for certain students/programs where scores are required.

        Exceptions

Student athletes

At this time, because of NAIA eligibility rules, student athletes are required to submit official scores in order to be declared eligible
                                for competition. These scores are submitted to both DWU and the NAIA.

International students

International students are still required to submit English Language Test Scores (TOEFL, IELTS, PTE) or College Preparation Test Scores
                                (ACT, SAT) for admission consideration. 

Homeschooled students without a transcript

Early Admission (Direct-Entry) Nursing Applicants

Students wishing to be considered for direct-entry admission into our nursing program should submit test scores. Students who choose not to
                                submit test scores during the application process may still declare a nursing major but will apply for entry into the program during their second year at
                                DWU.

Other outside scholarships

Scholarships offered by entities other than DWU may require a standardized test score, including but not limited to Dakota Corps, South
                                Dakota Opportunity Scholarship and others.
