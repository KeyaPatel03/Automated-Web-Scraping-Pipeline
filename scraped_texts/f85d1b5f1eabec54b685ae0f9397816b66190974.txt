DUAL ENROLLMENT

Dual Enrollment applicants are high school or home school students who want to take college-level courses at SMC.

Dual Enrollment Requirements:

To qualify for the Dual Enrollment program offered by Spartanburg Methodist College, you’ll need to meet the following requirements:

Be a high school junior or senior and at least 16 years old

Be enrolled in college preparatory coursework

Have a 3.25 GPA or higher on the Uniform Grading Scale

Course selection must be approved by the Registrar

You may enroll in no more than a total of 8 SMC classes and no more than 2 SMC classes per semester

You must maintain a 2.5 GPA or higher during each semester to be approved for the subsequent semester

SMC must receive the student’s final, official high school transcript with proof of graduation before SMC credit will be validated and released to any college or university

Complete an Online Application

Do this as early as possible to guarantee your place. 
GO TO APPLICATION

RETURNING STUDENT

Returning applicants are those who were previously enrolled at SMC but have stepped away from pursing their degree and wish to return; who have been placed on academic probation or suspension and wish to re-enroll; and those who have completed their associates degree with SMC, stepped away, and wish to return for the bachelor's degree. These students must reapply to be re-admitted to SMC. SMC must be your most recently attended college to apply as a Returning Student. If not, see the 
Transfer Student page

INTERNATIONAL STUDENT

NOTE: International students must complete the application process at least three months prior to their initial enrollment at the college.

Complete an Online Application

Apply early to guarantee your place. 
GO TO APPLICATION

Send Your Transcript

Provide a secondary or post-secondary transcript, with a specific date of completion, to our Admissions Office.

Secondary-Level Work

Provide an official English translation of secondary-level work (or any post-secondary work); official translation must be through WES or notarized by a translator.

Official Test Scores

Provide your official 
Duolingo English Test
, TOEFL, SAT or ACT score (a requirement only for international students).

Financial Support Affidavit

Submit an official Affidavit of Financial Support issued by a bank or notarized. The financial declaration must cover the net amounts necessary for a two year period (including any institutional financial aid).

Current Passport

Please include a copy of your current passport with your application materials.

Proof of Insurance

Proof of insurance is not required at the time of application but will be required before you begin classes at SMC.
