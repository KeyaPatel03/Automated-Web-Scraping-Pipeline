Here in the 
Office of Financial Aid
, our goal is simple: to educate our current and future students on all things Financial
                              Aid and provide support when you have questions or require assistance with those processes!
                              Our website includes the information you'll need to prepare for and complete the FAFSA
                              Application, the steps you need to take each year to receive the financial aid available
                              to you, a breakdown of all financial aid opportunities, financial literacy guidance,
                              and of course the knowledge you'll need to help pay for college!

We are here to help you navigate the world of Financial Aid — in person, at Tech events,
                              and online — so please contact us if you have questions!

The Bursar's Office

Tennessee Tech's Business Office collects tuition, fees, and other university-related
                              charges, including campus housing and meal plans. They also post refunds to students
                              with excess aid and pay students who work on campus. Visit their website below!
