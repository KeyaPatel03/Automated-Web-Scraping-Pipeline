Why UC

Choose your path and we'll help you create your own customized journey.

Undergraduate

Whatever path you choose, you’ll be ready to launch your career or accelerate directly into a graduate program. The University of Charleston offers several undergraduate programs across diverse fields, including business, health sciences, science, humanities, and leadership. With small class sizes and dedicated faculty, you’ll receive individualized attention that supports your academic success. Undergraduate students benefit from internships, leadership opportunities, and hands-on learning experiences that connect classroom knowledge to real-world careers. Whether you’re a recent high school graduate or a transfer student, we provide the resources and community to help you thrive.

Graduate

Our graduate programs are designed for working professionals and ambitious students seeking advanced expertise in their chosen fields. Programs in areas such as business, healthcare, and mental health counseling combine rigorous academics with flexible scheduling to fit your lifestyle. Graduate students at University of Charleston also have access to networking opportunities, career services, and faculty mentorship that empower them to achieve professional growth and personal fulfillment.

Enhance your leadership skills. Follow a life goal. Advance in your career. Change your life.

International

Students from 45 different countries call UC home – in fact, we’re the most diverse campus in the entire state of West Virginia. Our international student services staff work with international students from the time of application until graduation day, ensuring students have support throughout their UC experience. International applicants benefit from streamlined admission assistance, visa guidance, and cultural integration programs that make the transition to studying in the United States easier. University of Charleston provides support for housing, course tutoring, and academic advising to ensure international students succeed both inside and outside the classroom. While studying at UC, you’ll be part of a truly global community.

Military

We are proud to welcome veterans, active duty Armed Forces service members, and their family to the University of Charleston – we’re committed to meeting your unique needs. The University of Charleston participates in the Yellow Ribbon Program and accepts military transfer credits, making it easier for service members to continue their education. Dedicated staff provide guidance on benefits, scholarships, and flexible course options to support the demands of military life. Whether you’re stationed nearby, transitioning to civilian life, or balancing family responsibilities, UC ensures that military-affiliated students receive the respect, resources, and opportunities they deserve.

Online

Earn a degree from wherever you are with programs delivered 100% online. Our online programs are built with flexibility and quality in mind, offering the same level of academic excellence as our on-campus degrees. Students can choose from a variety of undergraduate and graduate options, all supported by expert faculty and accessible digital resources. With 24/7 access to coursework and strong technical support, University of Charleston online students can balance education with work, family, and personal commitments.

Academics 

Whatever your life goals, we have many outstanding programs to help you reach them. But UC offers something more – preparation to take on the world after graduation. In fact, a recent survey showed UC graduates were not only better prepared for life after graduation, but they excelled in the skills today’s employers are seeking, including teamwork, career management and global/intercultural fluency. Our Mission

The mission of the Office of Admissions is to identify, counsel, admit, and enroll students who possess the potential and motivation to be successful in their pursuit of intellectual growth in their chosen field of study as they prepare for a life of productive work, enlightened living, and community involvement.
