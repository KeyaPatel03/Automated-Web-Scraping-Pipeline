Meet Your Admissions Team

We know that choosing a college is about more than rankings, brochures, and campus tours. It is about finding a place where you will be known, challenged, and formed for the life ahead of you.
Our admissions team is here to help you discern whether Wyoming Catholic is that place. We will answer your questions, connect you with current students and faculty, and ensure you have everything you need to make a decision with confidence.
