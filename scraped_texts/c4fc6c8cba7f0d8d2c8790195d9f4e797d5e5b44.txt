Why Choose WSU Spokane?

Pursue your health sciences career with top-notch programs and small class sizes. As Washington State University’s health sciences campus, WSU Spokane offers interprofessional and experiential learning opportunities, providing you with the education needed to thrive as a health care professional.

Located on the edge of downtown Spokane and within walking distance of the Centennial Trail and the Spokane River, there are numerous dining options and outdoor or indoor activities near campus.
