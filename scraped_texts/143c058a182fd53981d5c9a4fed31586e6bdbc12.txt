Estimate Breakdown: 2026-27 Academic Year

Updated July 2026.

Use this page to estimate the annual cost of attending UT as a graduate student. The figures below include estimated tuition and university fees, required academic expenses, and living costs such as housing, food, transportation, and personal needs. Your actual costs will depend on your residency, academic program, enrollment, assistantship or waiver eligibility, living arrangements, and personal spending.

UT reserves the right to adjust tuition charges when deemed necessary. Official fees are set by the UT Board of Trustees in June/July of each year.
