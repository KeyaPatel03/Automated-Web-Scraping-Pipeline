World-class education, affordable tuition

At the University of Wisconsin-Platteville, we’re seeking Pioneers who want to change the world, make a difference, and create solutions. Whether your goal is to be an engineer, educator, or entrepreneur; a visual artist or veterinarian; an architect or archeologist, we offer an academic program that will prepare you to succeed.

We offer nationally-recognized programs in the fields of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. We have launched new, exciting collaborative programs, including nursing and physician assistant studies, that will train you for a career in healthcare. You can learn about the latest innovations in agriculture or gain the skills you need to start your career in environmental conservation and sustainability.

If you’re passionate about the arts, you can learn to master your craft through our programs in fine arts, music, theatre, and more. If you’re applying to college with a clear path to a career field, or you’re looking for the opportunity to discover what excites and inspires you, UW-Platteville is the perfect place to begin your college journey 
and our admission office will help you every step of the way.
