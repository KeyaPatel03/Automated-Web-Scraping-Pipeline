Costs & Aid

Your college education can help you discover more about who you are, your purpose, and how to live your values at work and in life. At WLC, we don’t believe your finances should be a barrier to having the high-quality, Christian college experience you deserve.

We’re proud that Wisconsin Lutheran College is recognized as a Best Value School by 
U.S. News & World Report
 and one of America's Best Bang for the Buck Colleges by 
Washington Monthly
. Our team in the Office of Financial aid is here to work with you to uncover financial assistance opportunities that fit your needs. They’ll help connect you with resources including federal student aid, scholarships, or grants.

Needs vary by student and program. You can find information specific to Undergraduate, Graduate, and Adult Accelerated programs below.

We want you to succeed at WLC. That’s why we’re dedicated to removing as many financial barriers — and the stress that comes with them — as we can for our students.
