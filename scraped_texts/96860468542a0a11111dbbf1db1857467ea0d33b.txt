BECOME A PATRIOT

At Francis Marion University, you’ll receive an extraordinary education and grow as a person as you learn the things you need to excel both inside and outside of the classroom. You’ll find a tight-knit community who interact as family, and a place where you’re challenged to achieve academic success.

Whether you’re studying in the classroom, learning in the field, or participating in an exchange program, you’ll acquire the knowledge and skills to prepare for a successful and rewarding future.

Academics

Francis Marion is home to over 75 programs and courses of study, with more programs added as the demand arises from our students and the areas we serve.

Every student at FMU receives a solid educational foundation through its core curriculum in the arts, sciences, and humanities, leading to well-rounded individuals who are problem-solvers, deep thinkers, and innovative regardless of field of study.

Vibrant Student Life

With over fifty different student organizations, fraternities, and sororities to choose from, FMU offers many opportunities for students to get involved.

In addition to student organizations, there are numerous campus events throughout the year for students to take part in including First Friday, intramural sports, yoga sessions, Greek Games, FMU’s Got Talent, paint and sup events, movies on the lawn, and more.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

At Francis Marion, we have worked diligently to keep our costs as low as possible for our students without sacrificing the quality of the education received. This year is our sixth year in a row with the same tuition. We’re committed to ensuring that everyone has the opportunity to receive the best education possible without breaking the bank. By choosing to invest in a Francis Marion University education, you’ll not only receive a powerful education, you’ll also be able to do it affordably by attending the university with the best educational value in South Carolina.
