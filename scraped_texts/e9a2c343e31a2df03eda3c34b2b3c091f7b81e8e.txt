Welcome to Vermont State University Admissions

We’re excited that you’re considering Vermont State University! 

Whether you’re beginning college for the first time, are planning to return to college to finish your degree, or want to advance your career through graduate study, we’re ready to support you every step of the way.

Empower your future with Vermont State

Academic rigor with the guidance of inspiring faculty to broaden your interests and potential  

Built-in opportunities to gain real-world experience and make professional connections in every program 

Low tuition plus scholarships and aid packages to help you reach your goals in a way you can afford  

A broad network of student support, from academics and wellness to career development, to ensure your success throughout your education and beyond 

And with VTSU’s flexible learning options — on-campus, online, and a mixture of the two — you can choose a program and learning method that works for you! 

Accessible, flexible, supportive, and affordable education.
