Apply to Beloit College

We can’t wait to get to know you.

As you scroll down, you’ll find all the technical information you need to apply to Beloit. 

But first, we want to make it clear that this is a human process about so much more than grades and test scores. We hope to learn who you are as a person 
and
 a student. We want to hear about your passions and dreams, your successes and disappointments. We will take into account the context in which you’ve worked, and any obstacles you’ve faced, and we won’t let them stand in your way.

Beloit College Application

High school transcript and Secondary School Report

We recognize our applicants come from a wide variety of schools and that curricular offerings vary. Most applicants have taken four years of English, three years of a foreign language, three years of mathematics, three years of laboratory science, and four years of history or social science.  However, we will consider your application regardless of your

course of study. 

The Secondary School Report is a form completed by your school counselor that provides Beloit College with an overview of your academic record.

For efficiency, students may submit unofficial transcripts at the time of application. Transcripts must include at least final junior year grades. Official transcripts will be required for verification purposes at the time of enrollment.

Optional:

Letter(s) of recommendation

While Beloit does not require letters of recommendation, we value the insight they give us into your ability, character, interests, and potential, as well as individual circumstances that may have affected you personally or academically. For that reason, we encourage you to submit at least one, and we will read up to three.

Most recommendations come from teachers in core academic subjects, but we will accept letters from teachers in elective subjects, extracurricular advisors or coaches, employers, college access counselors, clergy, family members, or peers.

Standardized test scores

Beloit is unabashedly test-optional for both admission and consideration for merit scholarships.

If you feel that your test scores reflect well on your abilities and would like us to consider them, please send them. We will consider your highest subscores from multiple test dates.

You may submit test scores via the testing agency (SAT code: 1059 / ACT code: 4564) or your official secondary school transcript.

Admissions counselor meeting

You may schedule a one-on-one meeting with an 
admissions counselor
 in order to learn more about us and to allow us to get to know you better.

Art supplement

You may upload digital creative files (poetry, photography, art, etc.) through the online 
Applicant Status Page
. You will receive login credentials within 48 hours of submitting your application.

While you may also mail physical items to the Admissions Office, please note that these items cannot be returned.
