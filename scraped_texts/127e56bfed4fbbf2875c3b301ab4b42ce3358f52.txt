Be inspired​

The sharpest minds from all over the world come here to build a life they love.

									Our students are go-getters who know life isn’t just about book smarts. They believe in growing as a whole person—mind, body, and soul—both in and out of the classroom. They’re unafraid to ask big questions about the world and themselves, constantly pushing for a deeper understanding.

So whether you’re an undergraduate student at the beginning of your college journey or a graduate student looking to broaden your options with an advanced degree, come to the University of Lynchburg to learn, connect, belong—and be inspired.								

Career Success

				Lynchburg graduates are job-ready with real-world experience, and 81.5%* of students surveyed 10 years after graduation were earning more than a high school graduate — that’s higher than the national average and ranks among the leading private schools in Virginia. 
*According to the 2024 State of the Commonwealth Report
