Introduction to Applying

The Best Way To Experience Lawrence Is To Live Like a Lawrentian.

At Lawrence University, you’ll join a community of Lawrentians who are creative, practical, and fearlessly curious. You’ll challenge yourself academically, explore ideas across disciplines, and apply what you learn in meaningful, real-world ways.

Competitive applicants typically pursue rigorous coursework available to them across core subject areas while demonstrating a strong academic GPA and showing curiosity beyond the classroom through research, creative work, global experiences, leadership, or service.

Test-Optional Admissions

Your ACT or SAT scores are not required for admission or scholarship consideration. If you believe your scores will strengthen your application, you’re welcome to submit them. The choice is entirely yours.

The following school codes may be used when sending scores electronically from the testing organization:

ACT: 4596

SAT: 1398

TOEFL: 1398
