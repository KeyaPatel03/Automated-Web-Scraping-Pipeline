Graduate School Policies

Graduate School policies and procedures are governed and approved by SDSU and the South Dakota Board of Regents. Utilize these policies as you complete your graduate degree. The Graduate School is here to support your success throughout your journey.

View Policies

Graduate Council

The SDSU Graduate Council supports the vision of excellence for graduate education by ensuring quality control standards across disciplines. The council also provides support for the Graduate School and addresses key issues critical to the success of graduate programs.
