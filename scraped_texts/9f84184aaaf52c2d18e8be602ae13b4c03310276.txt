Bryant & Stratton College Campuses in Wisconsin

For those looking for colleges in Wisconsin, the Greater Milwaukee area is home to two Bryant & Stratton College campuses. Whether you’re a student looking to head to class in Racine or even in the western reaches of the Milwaukee area at our Wauwatosa campus, Bryant & Stratton College has a Milwaukee campus location that will fit your needs, whether you are a recent high school grad, or mid-career looking to advance your professional impact.

The Milwaukee college campuses, located near scenic Lake Michigan, and tucked into a premier educational landscape alongside Marquette University, University of Wisconsin, Milwaukee School of Engineering, Milwaukee Institute of Art, provide students with academic programs in business, information technology, education, healthcare and more.

Students in the nursing program at the Bryant & Stratton College Wisconsin campuses are not required to petition or experience a wait list to enter the nursing program. Immediate acceptance allows students to begin taking nursing classes in their first semester in the LPN and ADN programs and in their second semester for the BSN program. With over 30 academic programs, our Wisconsin college campuses in Wauwatosa and Racine certainly have a degree or diploma program that will fit your interests.

The Wauwatosa campus also offers 20 intercollegiate athletic programs in the highly competitive National Junior College Athletic Association. Competing at the Division I and II levels allows the Bryant & Stratton College Bobcats to offer athletic scholarships to student-athletes.

Plus, both campuses offer robust internship opportunities through a wide network of corporate partnerships that offer a number of program and career-specific options for internships during your education or post-graduation.
