Explore and Discover

Memphis

In a city that has changed the world in more ways than one, we're driven to do the
                                                same. And so can you. The University of Memphis has been educating students from all
                                                over the world for more than 100 years. UofM researchers and faculty are leaders in
                                                more than 250 fields of study, which means a wealth of academic choices for you. In
                                                the heart of the nation's 28th largest city, learning isn't limited to classrooms
                                                or labs. Memphis is a hub of international commerce, culture and creativity. Whatever
                                                your passion, here you'll discover endless paths toward pursuing it.
