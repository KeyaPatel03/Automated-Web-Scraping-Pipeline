Applying to college is an important step, and at LeMoyne–Owen College, we’re committed to making the admissions process clear, supportive, and accessible. Whether you’re a first-time college student, transferring credits, or returning to school, this page will guide you through how to apply and what to expect next.

Who Can Apply

LeMoyne–Owen College welcomes students from diverse backgrounds and life experiences. Admissions information is available for:

First-Time Freshmen

First-Time Freshmen – Students entering college for the first time after high school. To be considered for admission, first-time freshmen should submit:

A completed application for admission

Official high school transcript

Minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0

Immunization records (after acceptance)

Transfer Students

Transfer students are students transferring credits from another college or university. Transfer applicants should submit:

A completed application for admission

Official transcripts from all colleges previously attended

A minimum 2.0 cumulative college GPA

Transfer credits are evaluated after admission

Readmit Students

LeMoyne–Owen College welcomes former students who are ready to return and complete their degree.

To apply for readmission, students should:

Submit a readmission application

Provide official transcripts from any institution attended since leaving LOC (if applicable)

Resolve any outstanding academic or financial obligations

Once readmitted, students will work with advisors and financial aid staff to develop a plan for re-enrollment and success.

Transient Students

Transient (or visiting) students are those currently enrolled at another college or university who wish to take courses at LeMoyne–Owen College for a specific term.

To apply as a transient student, applicants must:

Submit a transient student application

Provide proof of good academic standing from their home institution

Receive approval to take courses for transfer back to their home institution

Transient students are typically admitted for one semester.

Dual Enrollment

LOC is dedicated to empowering students in Memphis-Shelby County and the surrounding area with college access and experience. Our Dual Enrollment program allows eligible juniors and seniors to take college courses for credit while attending high school.

We currently partner with Hollis F. Price High School and actively collaborate with counselors and K–12 leaders to expand college readiness and ease the financial burden of higher education.

International Students

International students seeking admission to LeMoyne-Owen College on a student visa must complete an online application, pay the $25 nonrefundable fee, and have all transcripts—including those from previously attended colleges—evaluated by a foreign credential evaluation service and sent directly to the College at their expense ($50–$250). Applicants must also submit a notarized financial statement from a U.S. Embassy documenting at least $19,000 in support for one year, identifying the funding source and amount, along with an affidavit confirming that support. Documentation of two MMR and two varicella vaccinations, or proof of immunity, is required; some applicants whose first language is not English may also need to take the TOEFL exam.

Adults & Professionals

LeMoyne-Owen College’s ASAP Program helps adults 18 and older start or return to college with flexible virtual courses that fit around work and family life. Students receive a personalized degree plan, small classes, and dedicated advising from enrollment through graduation, with opportunities to earn credit for eligible professional, military, ministry, or workforce experience. ASAP students also enjoy the full college experience, including student organizations, leadership opportunities, and campus events.

Online Students

LOC Online offers a fully online, asynchronous pathway to a General Studies degree, allowing students to study from anywhere with accelerated coursework and virtual academic support. ASAP provides returning adult students with live virtual classes on a consistent schedule, combining real-time instruction, interaction, and accountability. Both programs help students pursue their degree while balancing work, family, and other responsibilities.
