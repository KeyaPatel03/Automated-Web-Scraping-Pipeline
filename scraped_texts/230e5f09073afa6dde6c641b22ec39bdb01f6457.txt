One Big Beautiful Bill Updates

Financial Aid Changes in 2026-2027

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (H.R.1), was signed into law by President Trump on July 4, 2025. The OBBBA brings important changes to federal financial aid that may impact both undergraduate and graduate students. These updates involve federal loan limits and requirements, repayment options, and Pell Grant eligibility calculations.

We understand that changes to financial aid policies can raise questions. The Office of Financial Aid is carefully monitoring these developments and will update this page as new information becomes available. The details on this 
page
 reflect the most current guidance provided to financial aid administrators.

What does this mean for you?

If you’ve already filled out
 the 2026-27 FAFSA – great! Shenandoah will receive the results of your FAFSA and send you an award package. Accepted new students will receive the financial aid package within 2 weeks of us receiving their FAFSA. Returning students will receive their aid package starting after final grades are posted in May.

If you haven’t filled out 
the 2026-27 FAFSA – it’s not too late! Applying early allows you the best opportunity to be considered for the most types of financial aid possible.
