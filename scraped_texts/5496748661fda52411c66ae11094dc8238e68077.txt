When to Apply

Carthage offers rolling admissions. There is no deadline to apply! Applications are reviewed as they become complete. It’s still best to apply early! If you apply early, we review your application early, and notify you of your acceptance early. You’ll also receive information about Carthage’s generous scholarships well before the Dec. 3 scholarship application deadline.

The Carthage application is always non-binding. This means applying and being admitted does not commit you to attend Carthage.
