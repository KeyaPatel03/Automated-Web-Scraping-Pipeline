Southern Utah University Works to Keep Education Affordable

As a public university, Southern Utah University is one of your most affordable degree options for undergraduate and graduate students. It is also a university known for the quality of its degree programs and for the individual attention students receive from faculty who are widely considered experts in their individual fields. You get a lot of value for your investment in education when you pursue your associate, bachelor’s, or master’s degree at SUU.

This information will help you determine what it will cost in tuition, fees, and other expenses to attend Southern Utah University. Please know that there are 
scholarships
, grants, and 
loans
 available to help make a Southern Utah University education even more affordable. We encourage you to use the 
Net Price Calculator
 to gain a more personalized estimate of the cost to pursue your degree at SUU. You can also 
estimate costs for International Students
.

Tuition and fees are subject to change from year to year.
