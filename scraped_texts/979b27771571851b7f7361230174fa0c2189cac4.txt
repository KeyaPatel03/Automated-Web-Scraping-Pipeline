Welcome to Graduate Admissions at DSU

At Dakota State University, our graduate programs are designed to help you advance your career and achieve your goals. DSU offers a nationally recognized education focused on quality, research, flexibility, and affordability, supported by expert faculty and cutting-edge research.
With strong global partnerships and programs built for working professionals, DSU provides flexible scheduling, online options, and affordable tuition—preparing you for success in today’s technology and cyber-driven workforce.
