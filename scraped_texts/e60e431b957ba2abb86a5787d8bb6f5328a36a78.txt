Why Choose VMI?

Virginia Military Institute develops leaders of character through a challenging academic, military, and leadership experience unlike any other college. With nationally recognized academics, a close-knit Corps of Cadets, and graduates who lead in every profession, VMI prepares you for success long after graduation. 

The VMI Difference

Exceptional Academics
 – Earn a nationally respected undergraduate degree in engineering, the sciences, humanities, or social sciences while learning from dedicated, award-winning faculty in small classes that foster mentorship and individualized support.  

Leadership Through Experience
 – Develop the confidence, discipline, and leadership skills employers and graduate schools value through VMI’s unique military environment and the nation’s largest ROTC program.  

A Community Like No Other
 – Join a close-knit Corps of Cadets built on honor, integrity, self-discipline, and the lifelong friendships forged through the “Brother Rat” experience.  

An Investment in Your Future
 – VMI offers scholarships, grants, and other financial aid opportunities to help make a world-class education more affordable for qualified students.
