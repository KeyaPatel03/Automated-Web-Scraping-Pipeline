Non-Degree Seeking

Undergraduate and Graduate

Non-degree seeking students are students who enroll at the university but have not
                              applied for or been accepted into a degree program. Non-degree seeking students plan
                              to take courses or complete certificates for interest and professional development.
                              Graduate courses and certificates are open to non-degree seeking students who have
                              already completed an undergraduate degree. Students who want to continue to pursue
                              their education are able to apply certificate credits towards most Westminster graduate
                              degrees. 

LEARN MORE ABOUT NON-DEGREE SEEKING STUDENTS

Returning to Westminster

Undergraduate 

Returning students were enrolled at Westminster and stopped attending for 3 consecutive
                              semesters (not including Summer term) without receiving an approved leave of absence.
                              To reenroll, students must complete the returning student application.
