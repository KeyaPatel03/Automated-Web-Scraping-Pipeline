See What Rhodes Offers 

Rhodes offers 130+ student clubs, organizations, and intramural and club sports, and is home to 23 men’s and women’s NCAA Division III intercollegiate teams. Approximately 47% of the student population ranked in the top 10% of their high school graduating class. Within one year after earning their Rhodes degree, 99% of our graduates are employed, attend graduate school, or volunteer full-time.
Our hometown of Memphis offers a vast array of opportunities for internships and employment, or just plain fun! Known as the Home of the Blues and the Birthplace of Rock 'n' Roll, Memphis is also home to world-renowned medical and research facilities, such as St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital (a partner of ours), and several Fortune 500 companies. On any given day, you can take in a live concert, enjoy a delicious meal at locally owned restaurants, tour cultural museums, or stroll one of many hiking trails. In your free time, you can also enjoy an NBA game, see a play or musical at the Orpheum, explore the Memphis Zoo (across the street from campus), or find your own special spots to enjoy. Our vibrant city is a substantial part of the Rhodes experience. Come see why Rhodes – and Memphis – are a place like no other!
