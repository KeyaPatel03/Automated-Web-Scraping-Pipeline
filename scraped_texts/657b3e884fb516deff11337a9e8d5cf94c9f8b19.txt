Office of Financial Aid

          ALL OFFICES & SERVICES

We’re here to help.

Emory & Henry works to keep student costs manageable, while maintaining a high quality, transformative educational experience.

Emory & Henry offers financial assistance to help students and families afford and achieve the student’s educational goals. The financial aid process is new to most families and at times may be confusing. Emory & Henry’s financial aid staff is here to help make the financial aid process as simple as possible.
