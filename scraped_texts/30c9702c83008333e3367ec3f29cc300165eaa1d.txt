Admissions & Aid

We won’t let you settle for second best.

Wheeling University provides an environment where students are held to higher standards and surrounded by a close group of unique and driven individuals. Our faculty is dedicated to educating men and women for others, and our students are always encouraged to get involved and supported in reaching their goals. Whether you’re a high school senior, international student, transfer student, or looking at our graduate and professional programs, WU can be the place for you. Explore our website and request information to learn more, or schedule a visit to come see for yourself. We look forward to welcoming you to the Cardinal family!
