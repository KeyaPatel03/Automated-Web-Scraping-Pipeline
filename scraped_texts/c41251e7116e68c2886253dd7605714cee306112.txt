Submitting an Arts Portfolio to the University of Richmond: A Guide for Applicants

Submitting an arts portfolio with your University of Richmond application is optional, and it can be a powerful way to showcase your creative strengths and share what you want to continue doing in college. Below, you’ll learn when to submit a portfolio, who reviews it, and how to select your best work in visual art, music, theater, or dance. Many students pursue their creative interests at Richmond through classes, performances, and ensembles without submitting an arts portfolio.

What Admission Officers Wrote About Successful Applicants to Richmond

We love to use the term “holistic admission” in the college admission process. It’s a widely agreed upon way to articulate a rather simple concept: building a class is like building an orchestra. To create a dynamic residential campus filled with many lived experiences, talents, and strengths, we’re assembling a group of students whose individual qualities come together to create something richer than any one person could accomplish alone.
