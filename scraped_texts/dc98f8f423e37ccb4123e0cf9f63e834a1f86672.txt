Department Values

Exceptional Care

We welcome the diverse experiences and perspectives of our students and faculty and
                                 denounce discrimination in any form. We strive to view each person as a unique individual
                                 who can succeed in a rigorous yet supportive learning environment. We value collegial
                                 collaboration as well as academic freedom and encourage the respectful exchange of
                                 ideas as a foundational element of the academic setting.

Exceptional Accountability

We promote professional and ethical standards of practice and behavior as we encourage
                                 both students and faculty to exhibit integrity in all aspects of the educational process.
                                 We view learning as a teacher-student-community partnership that highlights the roles
                                 of the student, instructor, and nurse as accountable agents to themselves, the University,
                                 and the communities we serve.

Exceptional Results

We employ evidence-based teaching and learning strategies to foster the development
                                 of caring nurses who promote the health of body, mind, and spirit in individuals,
                                 families, communities, and populations. Equipped with the ability to use effective
                                 clinical judgment, evidence-based nursing, and ongoing learning our graduates will
                                 be prepared to provide effective and compassionate service to members of both local
                                 and global communities.

Launch Your Nursing Career with Confidence

For more than 75 years, the UVU Nursing Program has prepared exceptional nurses through
                              a student-centered approach that combines rigorous academics, hands-on clinical experiences,
                              and state-of-the-art simulation learning. Students develop the clinical judgment,
                              technical expertise, and compassionate care skills needed to thrive in todayâs complex
                              healthcare environment. With strong partnerships throughout the community, dedicated
                              faculty mentorship, and a longstanding record of NCLEX success, UVU graduates enter
                              the profession confident, capable, and ready to make a meaningful difference in the
                              lives of their patients and communities.
