Proof of Lawful Presence (EVEA)

The University of Tennessee, Knoxville, requires all students who accept an offer of admission and confirm enrollment to complete proof of lawful presence (EVEA) verification requirements.

A Tennessee law called the Eligibility Verification for Entitlements Act (EVEA) requires UT to verify that individuals age 18 or older seeking a state public benefit — such as in-state tuition, state-funded scholarships, grants, and fee waivers — are U.S. citizens or otherwise lawfully present in the United States. UT’s policy extends this legal requirement to all students. 

Most students are verified through a citizenship-confirmed FAFSA. If your status cannot be verified by a completed FAFSA, please refer to the 
Proof of Lawful Presence (EVEA) information
 regarding the documents and document submission process you will need to complete prior to the start of classes.

How do I confirm enrollment?

Log into your Go Vols Portal and click the “Confirm my enrollment” checklist item to pay the non-refundable and non-transferrable $250 confirmation deposit.

If I decline enrollment after I pay the confirmation deposit, can I get a refund?

No, our confirmation deposit is non-refundable.

Why do I need to submit my final high school transcript and/or official test scores?

It is important for the university to receive your final high school transcript and official test scores to verify the self-reported information you provided on your application, because our admission decisions are based on self-reported information. Official transcripts and test scores provided by your high school and directly from your testing agency are used to confirm college readiness and finalize admission decisions and any aid you are offered.  

If we receive official information that is different from what you self-reported on your application, this will cause your offer of admission to be rescinded and lose any financial aid you have been offered. 

This can include but is not limited to: 

Being unable to provide official test scores which match prior submitted information 

Drastic drop in grades 

Negative grades in Senior year (12th grade) 

Dropping coursework 

I submitted my high school transcript and test scores on my Go Vols Portal. Why do I have to do it again?

Undergraduate Admissions must be able to verify information you self-reported on your application using your final high school transcript and official test scores to finalize your financial aid and admission to UT.

What happens if I do not submit my final high school transcript and/or official test scores?

If we do not receive your final high school transcript and official test scores, you will not receive scholarships or financial aid and risk losing your admission status.

How do I turn in my final high school transcript?

Your final high school transcript will need to come directly from your high school guidance counselor.

May 15

You will know we have received your final high school transcript when the checklist item on your 
Go Vols Portal
