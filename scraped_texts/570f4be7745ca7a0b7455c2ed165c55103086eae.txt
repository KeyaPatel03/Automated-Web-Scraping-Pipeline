Financial Aid Information

The Office of Financial Aid is responsible for making all financial aid awards.
We strongly recommend that the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) be submitted to receive maximum consideration for all types of student aid. The FAFSA is available online at 
studentaid.gov
. 
Please visit the Office of Financial Aid website for more information about the student aid process and for specific deadlines.

Office of Financial Aid

Deferral of Admission

Requests for deferral are subject to administrative review. Students may only submit a deferral request if they have deposited and the requested new entry term is within the same academic year as the original admission.
To request a deferral of admission, please contact your admissions counselor.
International students are ineligible for deferral.

Have questions?

Your Norwich Admissions Counselor is personally assigned to you and is there to support you at an individual level through the entire admissions process all the way to your arrival on campus. 
Please reach out with any questions you have about the Norwich process or experience.
