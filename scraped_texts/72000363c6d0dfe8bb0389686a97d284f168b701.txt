      Graphic Design

Graphic Design at RISD (RISD GD) pushes on the boundaries of the discipline. Here you will communicate ideas visually, play with the tools of the field, frame points of view and messages, build community and embrace a fluid, networked culture.
Through our degree programs, RISD GD provides the knowledge and skills for you to expand and challenge the field. Supported and encouraged by faculty, you design and follow a personal path forward in creative and professional practice.

Degree programs

Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA)
The communications principles and problem-solving strategies you acquire in the Graphic Design BFA provide you the foundation to build an informed, versatile and impactful design practice.
Master of Fine Arts (MFA)
By encouraging you to respond nimbly and creatively to a wide range of communications challenges, the Graphic Design MFA program prepares you to thrive in a constantly changing field.

What we make

In the studio students learn the fundamental value of typography, imagery, grids, systems and more in the course of creating everything from traditional books, posters, logos and websites to apps, interactive texts and other digital media.

      The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over the Lazy Dog — RISD GD Senior Show 2026

Inspired by the pangram that gives the show its name, the 2026 senior exhibition celebrates the quick, iterative experiments that lead graphic designers to critical creative decisions about type and form.

      Expiration Date — RISD GD Triennial 2024

A consideration of the harmonious relationship between graphic design and temporality, and a challenge to the fleeting existence of images and messages in the digital age.

Image

      For Reference Only — RISD GD MFA Biennial 2025

A collective provocation to see beyond the surface of a work, to read the subtext of what makes up graphic design—
For Reference Only 
invites viewers to take a second glance and discover something you’re moved to sit with.
