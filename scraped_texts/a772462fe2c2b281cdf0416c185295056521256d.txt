Welcome to East Tennessee State University

For over 100 years, students from around the world have moved to our beautiful region
                     of northeast Tennessee to attend East Tennessee State University, all for a common
                     goal: to achieve the dream of earning a college degree. Presently, over 400 international
                     students call our campus home. We invite you to learn more about ETSU as you begin
                     this exciting journey.
