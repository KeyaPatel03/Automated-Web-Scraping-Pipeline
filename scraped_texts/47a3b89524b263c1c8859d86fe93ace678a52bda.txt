Financial Aid

Financial Aid And Scholarship 

Here at FHU, quality education goes hand in hand with affordability. We offer personalized financial aid options in the form of grants, scholarships, student employment and loans. Our staff carefully considers each student’s unique needs and circumstances to provide the most advantageous aid package possible. Additionally, we encourage all students to submit their FAFSA by the priority date to ensure they don’t miss out on any limited funding opportunities. Rest assured, we are committed to helping you financially through your college journey.
