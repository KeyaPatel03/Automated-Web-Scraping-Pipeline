ANNOUNCEMENTS

New Location

The Office of Financial Aid has relocated to Ikenberry Hall. 
We are looking forward to the increased accessibility for students and families. Please stop by to see us in our convenient location on the second floor, across from the Business Office.

FAFSA Required

Shepherd University requires the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) to be on file for all institutional aid (Admissions, Academic, Honors, Athletic, etc.). Complete the 2026-2027 FAFSA now on 
StudentAid.gov
 using our school code 003822. 
International students are the only exception to this policy.

Checking On Your Upcoming Financial Aid Package

Have you accepted your financial aid and completed all of the necessary steps to ensure that your financial aid will disburse on time? The 
Financial Aid Checklist
 provides step-by-step instructions to ensure that you have completed all of the necessary requirements so that there will be no delay in receiving your payment.
