In order for your federal loans to pay to your student billing account, you must complete the entrance counseling and master promissory note (MPN).  Click below to learn how the WLU Financial Aid Office can help.

Professional Judgment
Your eligibility for WLU financial aid is calculated based on the information provided on the FAFSA.  WLU’s Office of Financial Aid understands that a student’s circumstances can change.  Our financial aid office has the authority to utilize a professional judgment to adjust the student’s FAFSA on a case-by-case basis.
