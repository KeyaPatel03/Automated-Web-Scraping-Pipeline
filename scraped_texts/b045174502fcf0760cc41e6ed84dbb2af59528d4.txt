Get a Head Start at CWU

It’s never too early to begin your studies. We support your academic goals by creating two pathways for high school students in Washington State to start learning at CWU. 

Our 
College in the High School (CiHS) program
 empowers ambitious high school students in grades 9–12 to get a jump start on their college education. Students can enroll in CWU courses taught by approved high school instructors to challenge themselves without leaving their high school campus. To ensure top-quality education, the CiHS program received national accreditation from the 
National Alliance of Concurrent Enrollment Partnerships (NACEP)
.

CWU’s 
Running Start
 allows eligible juniors and seniors to take tuition-free college courses at the Ellensburg and Sammamish campuses or online. Upon completing these CWU classes, they can transfer the credits to fulfill high school graduation requirements and to other colleges or universities.
