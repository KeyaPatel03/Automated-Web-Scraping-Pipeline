At the University of Washington, we believe every student, regardless of economic background, should have the opportunity to pursue a world-class education. Our mission is to expand educational access and opportunity by helping bridge the gap between the cost of attendance and a family’s available resources. Through scholarships, grants, loans, and work-study programs, we are committed to making a UW education as affordable as possible. Whether you attend our Seattle, Tacoma, or Bothell campus, you’ll find resources and support designed to help ensure finances are not a barrier to achieving your academic goals. Throughout this site, we’ve gathered all the essential information, forms, and resources in one friendly place.

 Don’t know where to start? Read through our financial aid overview page to receive guidance tailored to your student type. Get started by clicking on your academic level from the buttons below.
