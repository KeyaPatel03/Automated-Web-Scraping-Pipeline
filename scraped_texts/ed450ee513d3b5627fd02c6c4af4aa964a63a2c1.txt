Schedule Your Visit

This 30 to 45-minute campus tour is perfect for a quick look to see what Welch College is all about. You can always come back later for a longer visit.

This 3 to 4-hour personal visit is for the serious student who desires to get more info. After a tour, you will attend chapel and class, then meet with Financial Aid and/or an athletic coach.

Pack a suitcase for this 24-hour event for high school seniors, serious juniors, and undergrad transfers. Local students are welcome to sleep at home rather than in the dorms.

This is also a 24-hour conference but we allow 9-10 graders to join the juniors and seniors (dorm space is limited to 11-12 graders). This is 
ideal for church and school groups who are coming from a far distance.

Scholarships

Welch College is one of the most affordable colleges in Tennessee!

All Welch students receive a discounted tuition rate, and most Welch students receive some form of assistance. The Office of Financial Aid is your best resource for questions about scholarships, loans, and prices.
