Explore Tuition and Aid

We have lowered our undergraduate tuition price to $19,990 beginning in the Fall of 2026 and launched the “Emory & Henry Tuition Promise,” a program that will cover the cost of tuition for Virginia students who meet financial eligibility criteria. Under the program, new students who are Virginia residents with a household gross income (AGI) of $85,000 or less and a Student Aid Index (SAI) of 3,000 on their Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) will receive federal, state, and University gift aid that will 
completely cover
 the cost of tuition. The Emory & Henry Promise applies to new undergraduate students and transfer students beginning Fall of 2026.

Emory & Henry is committed to making an excellent education accessible to every student and offers generous academic merit, performance and affinity scholarships as well as need-based financial aid that makes the cost to attend even lower. We want to partner with you to make your education as affordable as possible.

Our qualified 
admissions counselors
 and 
financial aid staff
 are available to discuss what scholarship and aid options are available to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

  Do I have to submit all documents at the same time as my application?

  No. Application materials can be submitted with or after submission of your admission application. If you do not submit all materials upon submission of your application you will be notified of missing requirements.

  Where do I mail my transcript(s)?

  You will need to request official transcript(s) be sent to Emory & Henry. Transcripts can be mailed to Emory & Henry’s address or requested electronically from their high school guidance office to be emailed to 
admission@emoryhenry.edu
. 
Mail your transcript(s) to:
Emory & Henry University
Office of Admissions
P.O. Box 947
Emory, Va. 24327

  Is SAT or ACT required for admission?

  We are test-score optional at this time. For admission to Emory & Henry University, a student should complete the application for admission and submit their official transcripts from high school and/or for any college or university coursework.

  What is your average GPA for admission?

  The average GPA for new incoming students: First-Year 3.5 or Transfer 3.3

  Do you accept application fee waivers?

  Applications for undergraduates are free to complete with either the 
E&H application
 or the 
Common App
. Therefore, Emory & Henry University does not accept application fee waivers.

  Do you give credit for AP (Advanced Placement) Test?

  Yes. Emory & Henry College offers credit for AP Test based on specific scores related to specific subjects. See a list of Courses and Test Scores in our Academic Catalog.

  What is the deadline to apply?

  Mid-July

  When will I hear back about an admissions decision?

  We offer rolling admissions and typically once all documents are received you will receive an admissions decision within 24-48 hours.

  What type of Financial Aid does Emory & Henry offer?

  At Emory & Henry, we’re committed to partnering with you to make your education at Emory & Henry University affordable. Visit the 
Office of Financial Aid
 web page to learn more.

  Does E&H accept the College Scholarship Service (CSS) Financial Aid Profile?

  Emory & Henry University does not accept the CSS Profile, we administer our financial aid process through the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA).

  Does E&H accept the Yellow Ribbon?

  Yes. The Yellow Ribbon GI Education Enhancement Program (Yellow Ribbon Program) is a provision of the Post-9/11 GI Bill that allows veterans to attend private schools and graduate programs that cost more than the state tuition cap. 
Learn more about Veteran Benefits
.
