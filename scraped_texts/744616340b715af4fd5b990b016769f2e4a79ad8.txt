Financial Aid & Scholarships

Meeting the costs of an undergraduate or graduate education is a major investment for many families. It requires planning, careful decision making, and a commitment from both you and your family. We believe that students from all economic backgrounds should have the opportunity to attend the University of Washington Bothell and that finances should not be a barrier.

Financial Aid: Autumn Quarter 2026

Your autumn quarter tuition charges will appear on your account on September 17th. Autumn quarter financial aid will disburse to students’ accounts beginning September 23rd. Financial aid and scholarships will pay towards your tuition bill first, housing second (if you’re in the UWB Residence Hall), and any funds remaining will be sent directly to you either by direct deposit or a by check which will be mailed to you.

In order to receive financial aid for the Autumn quarter:

You must have a 2026-2027 financial aid application (FAFSA or WASFA) on file.

You must be registered for autumn classes.

If you want full-time financial aid, then you must be registered in: 

12 or more credits for undergraduate students.

10 or more credits for graduate students.

If you register in less than full-time credits, then your aid will be adjusted or cancelled to reflect the number of credits that you have registered for.

Please note that 
all 
aid, including student loans, will be adjusted to reflect your enrollment status if you are going less than full-time.

You still need to be attending at least half-time credits (6 for undergraduate and 5 for graduate) to be eligible for the Federal Direct Loans.

Please contact our office to let us know if you are taking less than full-time credits: 
uwbfaid@uw.edu

If your financial aid has not disbursed check to see if:

You are registered in full time credits (12 credits for undergraduate or 10 credits graduate).

Your document review has been completed.

There are any holds on your account.

You can call during our regular phones hours to speak with a financial aid counselor.

Document Upload-Student DocuSign

Contact the Financial Aid Office immediately if your aid has not disbursed so that we can review your status.

You can still submit your 2026-2027 financial aid application even though it’s after the priority application date of February 28th. You will be considered for the Federal Pell Grant, the Washington College Grant/College Bound/Bridge Grant, and the Federal Direct Loans.
