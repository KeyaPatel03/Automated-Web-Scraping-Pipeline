Undergraduate Conservatory Audition, Interview & Portfolio Review Guidelines

First-Year, Transfer, Re-Admit Students

Admission to a Music, Theatre or Dance program includes being academically admissible, as well as successfully completing and passing the mandatory audition, interview and/or portfolio review process. 
All confirmation materials can be accessed in the Application Portal and via the email listed on the application.

Complete the Undergraduate Application Process

Complete the 
First-Year Undergraduate Admission Application
 or the 
Transfer Undergraduate Admission Application

Submit official high school and/or college transcripts from each institution attended. Test scores are 
not required.

Submit artistic résumé

Submit headshot. Professional photos are welcome, but not required. A candid shot or senior yearbook photo is fine.

Musical Theatre applicants must also submit an additional $50 audition fee.

Admission decisions are not made until all application materials are received and the audition has been completed. Conservatory applicants must successfully pass their audition, interview, and/or portfolio review (if applicable) and complete the music skills assessment and written theory diagnostic (if applicable) in order to be admitted.

Fall Semester/Spring Semester Applications

Shenandoah Conservatory accepts applications and admits students to all conservatory programs in the Fall (August) semester. Shenandoah accepts applications for 
some
 programs for the Spring (January) semester depending on availability. Applicants are encouraged to contact Admissions to determine if their program of interest has openings for the spring semester.
