EMBRACE 
THE LEGACY

Morris College is a proud, faith-centered HBCU committed to developing strong minds and character. For over a century, it has equipped generations of students with the tools to serve, lead, and uplift their communities. From educators and entrepreneurs to pastors and public servants, Morris graduates continue to shape the world with purpose and conviction.

On our historic Sumter, South Carolina campus, you’ll be surrounded by a legacy of excellence rooted in spiritual growth, academic strength, and service to others. You’ll study with dedicated faculty who know you by name and challenge you to rise. And you’ll learn from influential voices across industries who visit to share wisdom and inspire bold thinking.

At Morris, your values matter as much as your vision. You’ll be celebrated for who you are while being prepared to lead in whatever path you choose. This is your moment. Connect with Morris College Admissions and take the first step toward your future.
