Cost & Aid

We’re Here to Make Your CIU Education Affordable

At Columbia International University , we understand that financing your education is an important part of your journey, and we are dedicated to helping make your studies as affordable as possible. Whether you’re an undergraduate, graduate, or doctoral student, we offer a range of financial aid options tailored to your needs. 
Our team is here to walk alongside you, providing personalized guidance and prayerful support as you navigate the financial aid process. We invite you to explore the various opportunities available to help fund your education and to ensure that cost doesn’t stand in the way of your calling. 
Let’s work together to turn your educational dreams into realities!
