Admission Requirements & Individual Program Details

School of Arts and Sciences

Mental Health Counseling

The Master of Arts in Mental Health Counseling admits students on a rolling basis for two starts per academic year (Fall and Spring). Transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a personal statement of purpose provide the documentation needed for application review. Our admissions staff will provide you with personalized guidance and support through the application process and assist you with the transition to enrolling in the program after admission.

School of Business

MBA

Business & Data Analytics  

Admission to graduate business programs at the University of Charleston emphasizes both academic preparation and professional experience. Applicants are encouraged to submit transcripts, résumés, and letters of recommendation that showcase leadership potential and career goals. The admissions team works closely with candidates to ensure they understand prerequisites and program expectations, making the process as smooth as possible. Prospective students also gain access to information on assistantships and financial aid opportunities designed to make a graduate business degree attainable.

School of Leadership

Cyber Security 

Strategic Leadership

Executive Leadership (Doctoral Program) 

Graduate leadership programs at the University of Charleston are designed for individuals ready to step into advanced roles across industries. Admissions staff provide guidance on submitting application materials, including transcripts, personal statements, and professional references. Because these programs often attract working professionals, flexible start dates and class formats help applicants fit graduate study into their careers. By outlining requirements clearly and offering dedicated support, the School of Leadership ensures that motivated applicants can confidently take the next step toward their leadership goals.

School of Health Sciences

**Physician Assistant Program 

Master of Science in Occupational Therapy

Doctor of Physical Therapy

Doctor of Nurse Anesthesia Practice

Admission to the School of Health Sciences is highly competitive, reflecting the rigor and demand of careers in nursing, physician assistant studies, occupational therapy, and physical therapy. Applicants should carefully review prerequisite coursework, clinical experience requirements, and testing expectations for their chosen program. The admissions team provides checklists and one-on-one support to help candidates prepare strong applications and stay on top of important deadlines. With resources tailored to healthcare-focused students, the School of Health Sciences ensures future professionals have a clear pathway from application to acceptance.

**The Physician Assistant Program offers a fast-track program for undergraduate students to transition seamlessly into the school.
