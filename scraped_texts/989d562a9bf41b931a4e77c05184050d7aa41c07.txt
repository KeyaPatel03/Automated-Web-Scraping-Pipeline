FIRST-YEAR APPLICATION CHECKLIST

You’ll need these two items to apply as an undergraduate student: 

Completed Ripon College application or Common App application 

Secondary school transcript

Must include grades from the first three years of secondary school

OPTIONAL APPLICATION MATERIALS

These items are not required for your application, but we’ll happily accept them if you have them.

Secondary school counselor recommendation

Additional letters of recommendation

ACT or SAT results

We will consider your test scores if you choose to submit them.

Scores sent from your high school counseling office or listed on your transcript will be considered official. 

Personal statement and college essay

Interview

An interview is required for students who are homeschooled.

                        WHAT ABOUT STANDARDIZED TESTS?                    

Ripon is a test-optional college. If you have taken the ACT or SAT and would like to include your scores, we are happy to consider them as part of your application.

                        I’VE APPLIED TO RIPON. WHAT’S NEXT?                    

Congrats on applying! While you wait for our decision, there are a few things you can do. You can come take a tour, apply for scholarships, apply for financial aid or reach out to our admissions counselors with questions.

                        WHAT SCHOLARSHIPS ARE AVAILABLE?                    

We strive to recognize our students’ extraordinary talents and abilities through financial assistance. When you apply, you’ll automatically be considered for merit-based academic scholarships. 
Explore all our scholarship opportunities, from academic to needs-based awards, here. 

YOUR EXTRAORDINARY FUTURE IS POSSIBLE

We strive to make transformative education affordable for all students. That’s why all our students receive some sort of financial assistance. From scholarships to financial aid, we can help you find a path to Ripon that is affordable for you.
