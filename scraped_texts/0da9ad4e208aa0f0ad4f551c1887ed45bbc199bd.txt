Our Financial Aid Commitment

Talented and dedicated students from all backgrounds should have the opportunity for a Middlebury education and the futures it opens.

Middlebury is tuition-free for families with annual incomes up to $175,000. 

We meet all admitted students’ demonstrated financial need.

Almost all of our financial aid is institutional grants and scholarships which do not have to be repaid.

Financial aid includes all books and supplies. Higher need students also qualify for student health insurance and a new laptop.

  We’re here to help you make a Middlebury education affordable.
