Admissions Details

                  Undergraduate Programs

Bastyr University offers two undergraduate programs.  

Browse Undergraduate Programs 

Prerequisites vary based on undergraduate program. All undergraduate students must complete the following prior to admission: 

Complete 90 quarter (60 semester) credits from a regionally accredited college or university or its equivalent (only courses with a grade of C or higher are transferrable)*   

Achieve a Cumulative GPA of 2.5 and Prerequisite GPA of 2.0   

Complete the General Education and Program Prerequisite requirements  

Apply for financial aid and scholarships, and learn about Bastyr's work-study program.
