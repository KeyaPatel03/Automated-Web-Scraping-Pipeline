The College at Wise is proud to offer competitive financial aid offers to anyone with demonstrated financial need. Making college more affordable for students is part of our commitment to uplift our region through the power of education.

How to Apply for Financial Aid
Apply to the College at Wise.

Submit your application
.
File the FAFSA.
 Ensure that your parents’ and your tax returns are filed as soon as possible, as you’ll need them to 
fill out the FAFSA
. Request for a copy to be sent to UVA Wise. Our school code is 003747.
Receive your Financial Aid Offer.
 Once you’ve been accepted, you’ll receive a financial aid offer letter detailing the forms of financial assistance offered to you.  Then get excited: You’re going to college!

Keeping College Within Reach for Virginia Students

Any student from Virginia whose family has an income of $80,000 or less and who qualifies for admission will receive scholarships that cover tuition and fees.
