Financial Aid & Scholarships

The mission of the Office of Financial Aid & Scholarships at Morris College is to guide students and families with clarity and care in navigating educational costs. We strive to increase opportunity and foster student achievement by connecting students with financial resources, scholarships, and support services that make college more accessible.

GRANTS ARE FUNDS THAT DO NOT REQUIRE REPAYMENT

Grants are funds that do not require repayment. Awarded based on financial need, they are determined by completing the FAFSA. Examples include the Pell Grant and Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant (SEOG).

Learn More

SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITIES

Morris College provides institutional scholarships and curated lists of external awards to help reduce educational costs and empower students to succeed.

Learn More

LOANS

Educational loans, offered through the U.S. Department of Education and private lenders, can help cover expenses. Some loans require credit checks and minimum enrollment requirements.

Learn More

WORK STUDY

The Federal Work-Study Program at Morris College provides part-time, need-based jobs, enabling students to earn money for education costs while gaining valuable work experience. Funding is limited and awarded through FAFSA.

Learn More
