Undergraduate Financial Aid

Brown’s commitment to affordability has never been stronger.

Brown meets 100% of each student's demonstrated financial need.  And we do this without including loans in University financial aid packages. 
The Brown Promise
, along with our 
newest initiatives

We believe that making an impact and addressing critical issues in the world demands an undergraduate experience that reflects a diversity of views, backgrounds and life experiences.  We actively seek students from all income levels and consider financial need separately from the admissions process for most applicants.
