Admissions & Aid

Are you ready to be extraordinary?

A Claflin education is a well-rounded education, preparing you not only with the technical skills your chosen field requires, but also the analytical, critical thinking and interpersonal skills that will enable you to make your mark on the world. Our students have the potential to be visionary leaders. For admission to Claflin, a record of academic achievement is important but only part of the story. We look for passionate, motivated students with integrity, a strong work ethic and a willingness to serve.

What possibilities do you see—for yourself, and the world around you?
