Financial Aid

We help make our education as affordable as possible for anyone who wants to be a Knight.

You can afford Southern Virginia University

Already at less than half the average cost of private colleges, Southern Virginia University also awards more than $5 million in academic, talent, or need-based scholarships to students annually.

Our friendly 
Financial Aid Team
 will work with you one-on-one to help you navigate your financial options and maximize the aid you receive. Through their help, 95% of our students have received some form of scholarship or aid annually.

Interest-free Payment Plans
The majority of our students choose to finance their education by making interest-free monthly payments through our Student Accounts office. You can set up an interest-free tuition payment plan in your registration portal or by sending an email to 
student.finances@svu.edu
. We recommend setting up a payment plan as soon as possible in order to spread out the cost for the lowest monthly payment option available.
