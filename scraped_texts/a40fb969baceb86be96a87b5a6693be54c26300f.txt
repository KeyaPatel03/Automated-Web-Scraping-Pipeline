Paying for College

Evergreen offers one of the lowest tuition rates and friendliest credit transfer policies of any four-year liberal arts college in the country. We’ll work with you to uncover scholarships, grants, loans and other forms of aid to help lower the costs of tuition and fees. About 65% percent of undergraduate Greeners receive financial aid each year.
