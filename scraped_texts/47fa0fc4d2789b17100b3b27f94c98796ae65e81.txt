First-Time Freshmen

The world is changing in powerful ways. You deserve a college education that equips you to meet the moment with skill, grace, and wisdom. Wisconsin Lutheran College provides you with an academic and faith experience that prepares you to see your potential and purpose in everyday life and work.

You’ll delve deeper into your interests and discover new passions to pursue. As a student at WLC, you’ll gain the skills to tackle real challenges through service, undergraduate research, clinical experiences, and professional internships.

Your time is now

You’ll gain confidence as a bold Christian servant-leader, able to lead in ways that are powerful and world-changing. You’ll graduate with a meaningful advantage in today’s workplace, ready to step into your purpose and your place in the world.

AN experience beyond academic excellence

Small classes with faculty committed to developing your unique gifts
