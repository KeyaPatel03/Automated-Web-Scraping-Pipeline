Annual Security and Fire Safety Report

The 
Annual Security and Fire Safety Report
 (PDF) contains current campus safety and disciplinary policies, crime statistics for the previous three calendar years, and on-campus student housing fire safety policies and fire statistics for the previous three calendar years. UW–Madison will provide a paper copy upon request; please contact the University of Wisconsin Police Department at 
uwpolice@mhub.uwpd.wisc.edu
