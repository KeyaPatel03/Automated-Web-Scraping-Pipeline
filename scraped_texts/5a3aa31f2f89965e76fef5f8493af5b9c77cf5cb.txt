Meet Your Admission Counselor
Our admission team is here to help you at every stage of the college admission process. Your dedicated admission counselor can answer any of your questions. Your admission counselor can also help put you in touch with other resources across the university, including athletics and academic support services. We look forward to getting to know you and your family!
If your counselor is not listed or you are inquiring from a territory not listed (e.g., Northern Rhode Island and Boston Metro Area), please contact us via email at 
admit@rwu.edu
 and one of our Admission Counselors will contact you with the information you have requested.
