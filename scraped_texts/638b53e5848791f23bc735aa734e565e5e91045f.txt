Merit Scholarships

Based on both your GPA 
and
 ACT (SAT) scores together, you may automatically qualify for renewable merit scholarships—no separate application required.

Provost’s Scholar – $6,000 annually

Awarded to students with ACT score starting at 27 (1280 SAT) and minimum 3.5 GPA.

Dean’s Scholar – $3,000 annually

Awarded to students with ACT score starting at 22 (1110 SAT) and minimum 3.25 GPA.

Firehawk Scholar – $1,500 annually

Awarded to students with ACT score starting at 19 (1000 SAT) and minimum 3.0 GPA.

Rising Scholar – $1,000 annually

Awarded to students with ACT score starting at 18 (960 SAT) and minimum 3.0 GPA.

Martin Transfer Scholarship – $3,000

Awarded to Transfer Students with a 3.0 or higher GPA and 24 hours of academic credit transferring in.

Chancellor’s Scholarship — Competitive full tuition scholarship

Up to five are awarded annually. A 30 ACT score and a 3.7 cumulative GPA qualify a student to apply; a committee selects recipients through interviews. Meeting the minimum criteria does not guarantee an award. Students who meet the Chancellor’s Scholarship requirements but are not selected for it will receive the Provost’s Scholarship. Students cannot receive both the Chancellor’s Scholarship and the Provost’s Scholarship.

All merit scholarships are renewable for up to four years (eight semesters) of full-time undergraduate enrollment, provided academic standards for renewal are maintained. In some cases, amounts of the merit scholarship will be reduced to prevent the student’s total financial aid package from exceeding cost of attendance. Merit Scholarships do not apply to international applicants applying for, or who have, F-1 visas.
