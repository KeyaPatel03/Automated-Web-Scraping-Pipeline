A Culture of Self-Discovery

At TSU, we provide a space and a promise that you will be the person you imagine yourself to be; with top-notch facilities, leading faculty, talented students and a stunning campus we are Tennessee State University. We promote a culture of self-discovery. If you want to be a Tiger, you want to be challenged to think, you want to engaged to help serve, and encouraged to work hard to achieve all of your goals. Whatever you choose to study our various degree programs will provide you with a myriad of opportunities and the space to interact with a diverse cohort. By choosing to attend TSU you have chosen a lifelong path to success and an educational transformation.

Think. Work. Serve.

Announcements

Please note:
 An intent to enroll is nonbinding. This expression of intent helps the school plan for the academic year while allowing students the flexibility to change their minds without penalties. Submitting an intent to enroll requires no financial commitment but does allow students to be considered for merit scholarships. Ultimately, students have the freedom to evaluate their options until the final enrollment deadline (Fall – August 1st, Spring – January 1st, Summer – May 1st) ensuring they make the best decision for their circumstances.
