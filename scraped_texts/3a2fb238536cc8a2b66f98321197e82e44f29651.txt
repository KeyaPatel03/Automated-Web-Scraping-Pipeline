Application types

Please note, a nonrefundable application fee, necessary transcripts, and appropriate test scores (if not test optional) are required with your completed application to be considered for admissions. 

Freshman Application

If you are currently enrolled in your last year of high school, or you have graduated but have not attended college or university yet. Requires a $60 application fee.

Apply as a Freshman Student

Transfer or Second Degree Application

A student who has already earned a high school diploma or GED, and attended another college of university after graduating high school. Requires a $60 application fee.

Apply as a Transfer or Second Degree Student

Non-Degree Application

The following admission types are considered non-degree: Transient or Visiting Students, Adult Learners, Pre-College High School Students, Senior Citizens, CCU Complete students. Requires a $60 application fee.

Apply as a Non-Degree Applicant

Re-Admit Student Application

If you were enrolled at Coastal Carolina University as a degree seeking student and did not attend for either a fall or spring semester. Requires a $15 application fee.

Apply as a Re-Admit Student

International Application

Applicants who are not citizens or permanent residents of the United States must meet the University’s general requirements for undergraduate admissions to apply as an international applicant.

Apply as an International Applicant
