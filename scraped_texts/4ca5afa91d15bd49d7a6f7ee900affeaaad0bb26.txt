Visits

Beloit is a must-see on any Midwest college tour. Explore a diverse and unique city and get to know an innovative college known for putting students first.

Applying for Fall 2027? You’re eligible for a $1,000 Visit Grant!

Simply attend an official campus visit during the 2026-27 academic year, and we’ll automatically add a 
$1,000 visit grant
 to your Beloit College Financial Aid Offer.

Visit Options

There are numerous ways to get to know us. Choose the one that’s best for you:

Daily Visits

See Beloit when it’s best for you

Campus tours and information sessions are offered Monday - Friday year-round with additional options available depending on time of year.

Visit Programs

Experience all that Beloit has to offer

From Information Sessions to Open Houses, our visit programs offer something for every student and every step of the college search process.

Discover what makes Beloit a college that changes lives.

Admitted Student Events

Celebrate with us!

Join us for an Accepted Students Day to immerse yourself in the Beloit College experience, meet your future classmates, and settle into your college home!

Visit Reimbursements

Let us help cover the cost of a visit to Beloit.

Beloit College can reimburse up to $500 in travel expenses for prospective students visiting campus for the first time. Funds are limited and students are encouraged to request a reimbursement as early as possible.

Virtual Campus Tour

Explore Beloit from wherever you are

Can’t travel to Beloit, Wisconsin? Can’t wait to see our campus? Immerse yourself in the 3D Beloit experience and discover the most important places and spaces on our campus.

Group Visits

For schools and community-based organizations

Counselors and teachers interested in bringing a group of students to campus for exposure to Beloit College, the liberal arts, and higher education generally, are invited to request a group visit. If we have availability, we are always happy to host our colleagues and their students.

Traveling to Beloit

Located on the Wisconsin/Illinois border, Beloit is an easy drive from nearby cities including Madison and Milwaukee, WI and Chicago, IL.

Chicago, IL is accessible by bus for families traveling from the city without cars or flying into O’Hare airport (which offers direct flights to most major domestic and international airports), so Beloit is accessible from just about anywhere.

For GPS directions to campus, please use the address 701 College St., Beloit, WI 53511. Parking reserved for admissions visitors is available on College St.
